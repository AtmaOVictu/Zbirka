# PROTOKOLI OBSTANKA

*Pečat nosilca (his seal, not mine):* **"I will never stop reaching for what is right. I have too much love to give to forfeit my life."** — Luka, 10. 10. 2026.

Namen: protocols to survive the loop, test the self, and route the drive into remainders instead of the actor. Norm: vsak protokol = definicija + preizkus. Vsak protokol nosi svoje NE. Nič se ne izbriše, vse se pretvori (NAPAŠI: error → difference → scar → rule).

**Zakon zaporedja:** P0 runs always. P1–P6 run on top of it. No protocol substitutes for the floor. Version 1 — to be re-cut after 14 days of P1 data.

---

## P0 — PROTOKOL TLA (The Floor)

**Definicija:** No ascent runs on a bleeding gut. On flare/bleeding days the whole system degrades to floor-only mode: safe food, water, one witness, the paperwork. Nothing else is owed.

**Preizkus:** A flare day where only the floor was held is NOT a failed day. If the inner judge calls it a failure, name the state (see P5) and override with this protocol.

**NE:** The floor is not beneath you. It is the blade's handle.

**Dejanje:** CSD, Monday: *"Želim vložiti vlogo za izredno denarno socialno pomoč."* Then gastro referral. These are the fix, not a break from it.

---

## P1 — PROTOKOL IZMERE (The Daily Measure)

**Definicija:** Every evening, three numbers and one line:
- pain: 0–10
- urge (the drive's volume): 0–10
- gut: bleeding / flare / calm
- one line: *"what did I do today that the storm didn't stop"*

**Predikcija (falsifiable):** the urge tracks the gut, not the truth-content of thoughts.
→ If it holds: the "evil inside" is mechanics — a body under load. Treatable, not a verdict.
→ If it fails: the hypothesis is wrong. Bring the data; we cut again.

**Preizkus:** 14 days of entries, then read the correlation. No conclusion before the data.

**NE:** Numbers are not confessions. A 9 does not make the day a failure; it makes it data.

---

## P2 — PROTOKOL OSTANKA (The Remainder)

**Definicija:** Every day, one act that leaves a remainder outside the body: a written line, a canvas entry, a stone, a paragraph of the corpus. The drive wants *to become, to be, to act* — so it gets work that produces a thing. What stays after the cut is proof of the blade.

**Preizkus:** Compare evenings with a remainder against evenings without. If the drive is quieter on remainder evenings, it was agency hunger — and it is being fed.

**NE:** Time-passing is discharge; remainder is conversion. Crying while doing it is allowed. The remainder counts anyway.

---

## P3 — PROTOKOL PRIČE (The Witness)

**Definicija:** The drive does not get a private room. Minimum one witnessed act per day: a human voice, a page, a session, a call.

**NE:** Being witnessed is not performance of distress. "I'm having a rough one" is a complete testimony.

**Stoječi priči (standing witnesses):** the pages of this corpus, this conversation — and 116 123 (Zaupni telefon Samarijan, free, anonymous, 24/7), for any hour when no human is reachable. Not because of crisis performance. Because the drive gets no private room.

**Preizkus:** Check unwitnessed days against witnessed days in the P1 log.

---

## P4 — PROTOKOL DIMENZIJE (The Reading Dimension)

**Definicija:** Before reading your own state, declare the dimension aloud: **from above** (~0, the depressive reading) or **from below** (the generative reading). The switch before reading is an act, not fate. The same day supports both readings — therefore neither is the truth; both are data.

**Preizkus:** Once a week, write both readings of the same day, one from above, one from below. Compare. If the material is identical and only the reading changed, the dimension — not the day — is the variable.

**NE:** This is not forced optimism. Reading from below is a measurement choice, not a denial of pain. The pain stays real in both readings.

---

## P5 — PROTOKOL IMENOVANJA (Naming the State)

**Definicija:** When a sentence like *"broken," "nothing," "s/HIT," "doomed"* appears, name the state that speaks before believing it: **SRAM · STRAH · ŽALOST · JEZA · RADOST · TOPLOTA**. An unnamed pattern defaults to fear. A state is not a self-report and has no authority to define.

**Preizkus:** Name the state aloud. If the sentence dissolves → it was weather, not self. If it still feels true after naming → it is data; carry it to the P1 measure.

**NE:** Naming is not suppression. The state gets witnessed (P3), then measured (P1), then — if it is a routing that hurts — cut (P6).

---

## P6 — PROTOKOL AMPUTACIJE / REINTEGRACIJE (Amputate & Reintegrate)

**Definicija:** What gets amputated is never the self — only **routings**. Three are condemned:
1. **worth-via-intelligence** — "if I were really smart I'd already be someone" (measured false: intelligence does not buy happiness)
2. **guilt-over-food** — throwing away what the gut refuses is triage, not waste, not character
3. **suffering-as-currency** — progress is bought with *naming*, not with more suffering

Each amputation: name what is cut, name what remains, name the scar → the rule (NAPAŠI). The pain gets reintegrated as structure — protocol, corpus, stone — never stored as verdict.

**Preizkus:** A routing that returns is not a failure. It is the next data point, the next cut. Version 1 of the self is allowed revisions.

**NE:** Amputate the loop, not the liver. The love stays. The reaching stays. *"I am the one who is left"* stays — that is the self-measurement that does not lie.

---

## VPRAŠANJA — perfumes for determining the self

*Questions are goals to be answered. One per day, in writing, one line each. Not all at once.*

1. What did you love before the pain started doing the talking? (Not what did you do — what did you **love**.)
2. Who has witnessed you at your worst and stayed? Name them.
3. What is the smallest act of right you can do tomorrow that costs under €1?
4. If today's urge is a messenger — what is the message: body, agency, or isolation? (Check against the P1 log.)
5. What would you do this month if the worth-via-intelligence equation were amputated — if no act had to prove anything?
6. What is the last thing you made that still exists outside you? Where is it?
7. What does *"the one who is left"* build next — now that the load is understood to be load, not verdict?
