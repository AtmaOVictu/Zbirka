## 4. Verification layers

These states are independent and must not be collapsed:

- `TEXT_PRESENT`: text is present in a named artifact.
- `IMPLEMENTED`: an identified program or procedure implements the requirement.
- `TESTED`: a named test ran on an identified target and yielded a stored result.
- `INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED`: a reviewer distinct from the executor inspected an explicitly stated scope and evidence.
- `PUBLISHED`: an exact artifact is available at a cited publication location / immutable identifier.
- `LEGAL_REVIEWED`: a qualified review of a stated scope was completed and recorded.

A SHA-256 digest only identifies bytes relative to a known reference. It does not by itself establish who authored or approved the bytes, whether the claims inside are true, or whether a license is enforceable.
