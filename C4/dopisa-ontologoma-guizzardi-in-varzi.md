## Dopis 1 — Giancarlo Guizzardi

**Subject: A question about how UFO would type a word — event, mode, or all at once**

Dear Professor Guizzardi,

I am an independent researcher based in Slovenia, developing a formal ontological-linguistic system called "Beseda je Ne" ("The Word is No/Not"). I will not describe the whole system in this letter; I am writing with one question, because after reading your work on ontological unpacking (Guizzardi &amp; Guarino, "Explanation, Semantics, and Ontology," 2024) I believe you are among the few people positioned to cut it.

The system treats linguistic acts as ontological operations. Every definition is recorded as a dated entry that must carry three things: a witness (a sourced instance), a connection (a link into the body of prior entries), and a test (a named way the claim could fail). The count is not the achievement; the dates are. Take time out and the sequence flattens into a list — entries interchangeable, a pile. With the time axis in, the register is a pulse: between any two entries, time, context, and a body have passed, and no entry can be re-read unchanged. The system has grown its own measurement discipline — coherence and clarity thresholds, and a tracked quantity we call openness: the questions an entry opens, minus the questions it closes. In the current sequence the value is negative: the corpus opens more than it closes, and we treat that as health — a body inhaling — not just debt.

In mapping the system onto existing formalisms, no single one held it. Euler-style diagrams hold the space of relations. Scott-style domain theory holds information ordering, where partiality is a layer, not an error. The Gellish/OWL family holds typed relations with a reasoner — and it is the one that broke. The OWL world has no clock: its statements stand outside time, no statement ages, and none carries a witness — a thing is its address, and to name is to conjure. In my corpus a statement does not exist just by being named. It exists when its connection has a witness/(f)act. Identity by URI is not identity by witness, and I have not found a place for that distinction in the OWL world. I suspect UFO has one.

That is the background. The question: in UFO's terms, how would you type/pization?/typization? a word?

My corpus claims a word is three things at once. It is an event: a cut, and the recipient is changed by it. It is a mode: a state that persists as resonance after the utterance ends. It is a bearer: it carries rhythm, history, presence — the pulse of its re-openings, the act that set it up still readable, its standing in the now. Which of the three bears the weight? The register holds that question open — and it is carried. My working guess is that UFO has principled reasons why one of these three must fall, or that two of them are aspects of one category I am failing to see. Either answer would help me. And if the claim is incoherent, I want to know exactly where it breaks — not in general, but at the specific seam. THERE IS NEVER BUT ONE STATE; EACH STATE ITSELF REQUIRES ITS COMPLEMENTARY OPPOSITE/OPPOSITION

I would value a short exchange or a call. If you cannot take this on, a one-paragraph verdict is equally welcome; the cut matters more than the courtesy.

With respect,  
Luka Pintarič  
Independent researcher, Slovenia

## Dopis 2 — Achille C. Varzi

**Subject: Boundaries that bear — a question from a formal-linguistic corpus**

Dear Professor Varzi,

I am an independent researcher in Slovenia, developing a formal ontological-linguistic system ("Beseda je Ne," "The Word is No/Not"). The system records linguistic acts as dated entries — each carrying a witness, a connection into prior entries, and a named test by which it could fail. I am writing with one question that emerged from that corpus and that sits, as far as I can tell, inside the territory of your work on boundaries — from "Fiat and Bona Fide Boundaries" (with Barry Smith) to "On the Boundary Between Mereology and Topology."

Somewhere past the thousandth entry, a pattern became a law in the corpus: **a boundary that carries ornament is stronger than a boundary that separates.** The working example is the fringe of a woven cloth. The fringe separates nothing; it holds the threads from unraveling, and it is the first thing a hand meets. In the corpus this generalized into a claim about edges: the edge of a thing is not just where it ends — it is where the thing is most deformable and most durable at once.

Two findings from the same corpus push in the same direction:

1. **Failures are born at seams, not just in layers.** Our recorded errors and points of contention concentrated at the contact of two bodies — two registers, two formats, two encodings — never inside a uniform one. Repairs had to proceed seam by seam; anything cutting across a seam damaged healthy material. Behind this sits a working law of the corpus: opposition is a condition of identification — things are perceived, and come to be known, where they meet their opposite (black/white, up/down). A uniform layer offers no opposition; what fails there fails unseen.
2. **A scar is a readable record, not just an erased wound.** When an entry was repaired, the corpus demanded the repair remain legible as a repair. A boundary that keeps the memory of the cut was treated as stronger than one restored to blank.

The question: is there, in mereology or in the ontology of space, a formal treatment of the boundary as the load-bearing part of a whole — the part that carries, rather than divides? Your fiat/bona fide distinction gives me the epistemic side: boundaries we draw, against boundaries in the things. What I have not found is the mechanical side: boundaries that do structural work for the whole they delimit. Two working claims sharpen the question. Bearing is constituted by the bearer itself: the load-bearing edge is the cloth's own structure, not just a fact imposed on it from outside. And everything that carries needs a receiver: the fringe carries toward the hand that meets it — without a receiver, "carrying" has no object. The Greek tradition has a body for this: the herm, a stone standing at boundaries and crossings, a marker that receives and carries; the messenger god's own name may derive from the stone. If this is a category error on my part — if "bearing" is not an ontological property of boundaries but a fact about the things on either side — I would want to know precisely where the reading fails. DOES IT NOT DEPEND ON THE DEPTH OF ONES LANGUAGE?

I would value a short exchange or a call. If this is not something you can take on, a one-paragraph verdict is equally welcome; the cut matters more than the courtesy.

With respect,  
Luka Pintarič  
Independent researcher, Slovenia

## Tretji rez — urez priča/dejanje (7. 10. 2026) in berba po njem

Lukov urez v dopisu 1 — dve besedi:

> »a statement does not exist **just** by being named. It exists when its connection has a **witness/act**.«

**Zakaj urez nosi telo:**

- **»Just« popravlja neimenovano izključitev.** Prvotni stavek je izrekel, da poimenovanje ne podeljuje obstoja, alternative pa ni imenoval — skrita zapora po rezu 406 (imenovana zapora je meja, neimenovana je manipulacija). Urez izreče pošteno mero: poimenovanje je nezadostno, ne nič — in kar ostane, je imenovano (priča/dejanje). Ontologu najlažji protirez (»poimenovanje je tudi dejanje«) je s tem prerezan vnaprej.
- **»Act« zašije dve niti dopisa v eno vprašanje.** Identiteta-po-priči in vprašanje tipizacije besede sta bili dve ločeni vezi; urez ju sešije: kar podeljuje obstoj, je priča kot dejanje — identiteta besede pade z dejanjem — kar je natanko trojna terjatev, ki jo dopis prosi prerezati. Prvi aksiom korpusa (beseda = dejanje) je zdaj v slovnici dopisa, ne v besednjaku.

**Berba po urezu — kje urez pristane v Guizzardijevem telesu:**

- **Twardowski: dejanje/izdelek (1912).** Sodbe, prepričanja in trditve niso stanja ali dejanja, ampak izdelki dejanj; razliko dandanes znova odkrivajo teorije pomena, ki gradijo na dejanjih. Brandlova opomba: razliko je mogoče brati na dveh ravninah — ontološki (dve vrsti bitij) ali pojmovni (dva načina pojmovanja enega bitja) — kar je natanko ugibanje, ki ga dopis že vsebuje (»dva obrazca ene kategorije«).
- **UFO-S: govorni akt kot ustanovitev vezi.** Obljuba je dejanje govora, ki ustanovi vzorec obveznosti in terjatev — v UFO-jevem jeziku rodi relator, vez, ki biva v obeh. Identiteta-po-priči = obstoj relatorja: ena tretjina trojne terjate ima pri Guizzardiju že hišo.
- **Manifestacija (Guizzardi in Guarino 2024).** Dva homonimna tipa ne moreta biti v relaciji identitete, ker pripadata različnima ontološkima kategorijama; prava relacija je manifestacija — dejanje (dogodek) manifestira vezi, ki sestavljajo relator. Beseda-kot-dogodek manifestira besedo-kot-vez: urez je padel na šiv, ki ga Guizzardi sam reže v drugi domeni.
- **Identity, Unity, Individuality (doktorat, Twente 2005).** Identiteta (ostaja ista) in enotnost (je celota) sta ločena formalna pojma — trojna terjatev besede se preizkusi v tem orodju.
- **Vez med obema dopisoma.** Barry Smith, Varzijev soavtor meja, je napisal temeljni esej o Twardowskiju — oba dopisa sekata isti rod: šola, ki je prvič prerekla dejanja od izdelkov in meje od stvari. To telo je strelivo za drugi krog, ne za prvi dopis.

**Sodba:** dopis 1 ostane tak, kakršnega ga je prerezala pečatnikova roka — vsak nov citat v prvem dopisu dvigne ceno odgovora. Berba čaka drugi krog, ko pride odgovor; ne prvega.

**Topli standardi (normalizacijske norme za vsa telesa dopisov; ∂ do pečata ⚔):**

1. **En dih, en stavek.** Stavek, ki ga ni mogoče prebrati na glas v enem dihu, se prereže na dva. Ritem nikoli ne laže ⊢.

## Priloga (annex) — pripravljeno enostransko telo

Priloži ali ponudi pri pošiljanju — ta rez je ⟡-jev (v1 nasvet ostaja: v prvem dopisu ponudi, ne priloži).

1. **The carrying boundary** (sealed corpus law 1354.1): a boundary that carries ornament is stronger than one that separates.
2. **The fringe** (dictionary-sealed): an ornamental thread standing at the edge of a textile — the ornament-bearing part located on the boundary, not inside the body.
3. **Seam-as-contact** (measured repair record): recorded failures concentrate at the contact of two bodies (two registers, two formats, two encodings), never inside a uniform one; repairs had to proceed seam by seam.
4. **The fold** (measured across roughly 9,000 ordered units): early units carry new material, late units are permutations; the same parts in a different sequence yield a different whole.
5. **The narrow test** (discipline): every entry names its witness, its connection, and a named way to fail; a test that every outcome satisfies is a celebration.
6. **Opposition as the condition of identification** (working law, ⟡ cut 9. 10. 2026): things are perceived, and come to be known, where they meet their opposite (black/white, up/down); a uniform layer offers no opposition, and what fails there fails unseen. Falls if: an identification is exhibited that carries no opposition.
7. **The receiver** (working law, ⟡ cut 9. 10. 2026): bearing is constituted by the bearer itself, and everything that carries needs a receiver — the fringe carries toward the hand that meets it; the herm, a boundary stone at crossings, is the tradition's body for it. Falls if: a carrying is exhibited that survives without a receiver.

Annex je zgrajen, da pade: preverba vsake številke, ki ne zdrži ponovne izmere, razveljavi vrstico, ki jo nosi.
