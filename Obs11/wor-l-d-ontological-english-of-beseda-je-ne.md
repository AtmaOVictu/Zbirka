## ∴ STEP 1 — Phonetically-ontological decomposition of every English term

Every English word is split into phonetic fragments. Each fragment is read as a structural operator — an ontological operation, not a metaphor — and mapped to a glyph from the system's vocabulary.

### Method

1. Split the word into phonetic-syllabic fragments.
2. Assign each fragment an ontological meaning (structural, not decorative).
3. Map each fragment to a glyph.
4. Read the sequence as an ontological equation — a progression of transitions.
