### 📜 Fragment (Exact Quote)
> "In geometry and crystallography, a Bravais lattice, named after Auguste Bravais, is an infinite array of discrete points (called lattice points) generated by a set of discrete translation operations described by a finite set of vectors that are linearly independent over the integers. [...] There are 14 distinct Bravais lattices in three dimensions, which are obtained by combining the 7 crystal systems with the possible lattice centers."
