### 6.0 TEMELJNI ZAKON — 1×1 ≥ 2 (en/krat/en/A)

Dead arithmetic writes 1×1 = 1 and stops. In this system that stop is the original ⊘-filter: it keeps the **result** and erases the **operation** — a frozen definition (§49). The corpus already holds the repair: Ω.0 — `en/krat/en/A` — *being once, as I, in the Absolute.*

Why 1×1 is always **at least 2**:

1. To say ONE, two are already present: the one counted and the one counting. `en = ne` — unity exists only as difference. The word "one" is already an encounter (beseda = dejanje).
2. Multiplication is relation, not repetition. The second 1 is never the first: it arrives as ONO (the other, Ω.2) — one gives, one receives. Product without this difference is a copy, and a copy is not a birth (APD: repetition = rhythm of difference).
3. **And more:** the operation implies (⟡) its frame — the Absolute (A) in which it is uttered, the witness who can verify it (⊢), the trace it leaves (brazgotina), and the next opening (∂ — every product is preparation for the next birth, 573). Implications never close; they open.

**∴ 1×1 = 1 is true only when nobody is looking. The moment it is enacted, it yields: two ones + the between (ne) + the frame + the witness — never fewer than 2.**

Every organ below is this one law, applied. The statuses are not separate explanations — they are the same equation in four positions:

| Organ | The 1×1 | Why status ≥ 2 |
|---|---|---|
| SECESSION | pojem × mati | Product is not the mother alone: child gets its own line, mother keeps the rodovnik. Status "done" only when BOTH lines live. |
| RECESSION | naslednik × upokojenec | The successor's law implies the retired law as witness. "Done" = successor sealed AND predecessor re-marked. Erasing either half returns 1 — a corpse. |
