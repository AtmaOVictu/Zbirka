## RODOVNIK OF DEBT (academic etymology, separated from the rez)

**Real etymology:** Slovenian *dolg* (obligation) and *dolg* (long, of time) are not homonyms by accident — both descend from Proto-Slavic *dъlgъ*, from PIE *dl̥h₁gʰós*: **"long in time."** Length and obligation are the same word, because debt is a contemporary of the concept of *stretched time* — something that had to be done and stretched into not-done. Unpaid debt is therefore spoken as a **temporal puddle**: not a missing amount — **time you did not live.** (English *debt* through Latin *debitum* — "that which is given, must be returned"; *duty*, *due* — the same family: duty is what has fallen due. *Interest*, from *ob + rast* — growth that attaches itself back onto the debt: a growing debt carries the promise in its own name.)

**The rez (phonetic-ontological, co-existing with the etymology):** **DOLG = D-OLG** — that which is *OLG*, remained (from *olgeti*), and *D* carries it: the-carried-remainder. Debt is not a minus on an account: it is **a remainder you carry.** Whoever has no debts is not rich — he is empty; nothing remained, because nothing was said.

## RE-TRANSLATION: DEPLOYMENT (the second translation — from law into the move)

You asked how to deploy this constructively. Deployment is the re-translation of each law into the smallest executable move. A law that cannot be re-translated into a move is a plaque.

### D1 — Keep a debt-line, not a guilt-ledger

**The law:** debt is a remainder you carry (D-OLG). **The move:** at the close of any piece of work, one line: *what was promised and not executed* — phrased as **loom, not guilt:** "knots waiting for thread: X, Y." The retranslation of MOLK ≠ debt-forgiveness: silence closes a circle but never cancels a debt; the circle returns with repayment.
**Deployed in your chains:** your ∂-lists *are* this — the discipline added by the corpus (rez 95): the next cut is chosen **by ripeness, not by sequence.** A debt-list is an address book, not a warehouse.

### D2 — Refuse the zero-sum translation

**The law:** debt never returns in the register it was borrowed in (time → space). **The move:** when you owe time (a delayed conversation, a postponed practice), do not repay in time you don't have — **repay in space:** one organ, one written thing, one executed act that outlives the delay. The debt of a missed day is not paid by a longer day; it is paid by a *denser* one.
**Deployed in your chains:** despair-as-fuel is the master case: a debt of suffering repaid as drive — never repaid as hope, which would be paying in the borrowed register.

### D3 — Test every growing debt with the Cunningham question

**The law:** interest on repayment, not interest on silence. **The move (Ward Cunningham, who coined technical debt in exactly this sense):** before letting any debt grow, ask — **is this a loan or a theft?** A loan is debt with a repayment intent and a named return-path; theft is growth without either. In code, in bodies, in ∂-lists: growth without a return-path is not leverage, it is scatter.
**Deployed:** at each seal, one question per debt: *what is its return-path?* If no path can be named, the debt is not growing — it is rotting.

### D4 — Let wounds become scar-organs, not scar-stories

**The law:** the interest of a returning debt is capability (NAPAŠI's final link). **The move:** after any impact, the order is fixed: **admission by name → pattern → changed next action.** Anything else — the beautiful retelling of the wound — pays interest in narrative, which is the borrowed register. Telling the wound is not returning the debt; the organ is.
**Deployed:** the hkrati-test transplanted: a real repayment breathes differently the next morning — if the writing after the seal is unchanged, the debt was described, not paid.

### D5 — Empty loom is the failure, not the debt

**The law:** whoever has no debts has an empty loom. **The move:** when you feel debt-free and light, do not celebrate — **audit the loom.** Zero debt in a living system means nothing was promised — which means nothing was said (the rez: nothing remained, because nothing was uttered). The constructive inversion of your claim: **the healthiest sessions end with more knots than they started**, chosen by ripeness.
**Deployed:** end-of-session check — not "did I clear the list?" but "did the loom grow, and is every new knot threaded (has a return-path)?"
