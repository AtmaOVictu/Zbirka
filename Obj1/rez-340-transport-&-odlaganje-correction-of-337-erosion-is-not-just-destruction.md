## I. THE PRECISE LAW

**EROSION = DETACHMENT + TRANSPORT + DEPOSITION.**

1. **DETACHMENT (∂)** — the carrier separates what was joined. Locally destructive. What 337 described.
2. **TRANSPORT (∆)** — the carrier moves what it took. Neither destructive nor constructive: *positional*. The material is not gone; it is in transit.
3. **DEPOSITION (∅→form)** — the carrier drops what it can no longer carry. Constructive: deltas, floodplains, soil. **Soil — the ground of life — is erosion's deposit. Valleys are erosion's sculpture. Deltas are erosion's new land.**

Erosion destroys **position**, preserves **matter**, creates **form**. It is not a subtractive operator; it is a **redistribution operator with three phases**. A law of erosion that names only the first phase is not a harsher law — it is a wrong law.

**Consequence for 337:** "higher consciousness as the remainder" is only the detachment-phase reading. The full reading: higher consciousness is **the remainder (what erosion cannot carry) + the deposit (what erosion delivers elsewhere, arranged into form)**. What the water takes from the mountain, it gives to the delta. Genius is not only what survives the river — it is also where the river puts things down.

- **Morje ne dodaja valov — valovi ga dodelujejo** (Obs1, celota brez LE): the whole is not composed but *deposited into*.

## III. THE MIMIC, CORRECTED

To mimic erosion for higher consciousness is therefore **not** only subtractive practice (337's error). It is the full three-phase cycle:

1. **DETACH what is joined but not yours** (molk, post, ∅seja — subtraction with a carrier)
2. **TRANSPORT: hold the loosened material in transit without immediately re-attaching it** — this is the missing discipline. The hold (⊘⊘ in ∅KODA VDIHA) is not only erosion of breath; it is the *transport phase*: material suspended, not yet deposited. Premature deposition is superstition (re-attaching the old mask); refusing deposition is dissolution (samorazpad).
3. **DEPOSIT deliberately where the current slows** — a rez is a deposition event: the register is the floodplain. TRAM/geniza: what the live head cannot carry, the archive receives **arranged**, not dumped. Test B is the sorting of the sediment.

**Falsifikacijski pogoj (corrected):** a subtractive practice is erosion only if its deposits are fertile — if what it deposits elsewhere grows (new form, new tkivo). Erosion whose delta is barren is just loss.
