# VBL — The License Explained

**A public teaching guide · Not an official license release · Not legal advice**

*Based on the VBL text supplied by Luka Pintarič. This guide explains its principles and selected provisions; it does not amend, replace, canonize, or establish the enforceability of the license.*

## The roots

The Verification Before Assertion License (VBL) begins with six working principles:

1. **Measure, then speak.** Every claim should carry a verifiable witness. If it has not been verified, mark it as uncertain rather than presenting it as fact.
2. **Name every fall.** An error or harm should be named. A correction must not silently erase the history of the mistake.
3. **Never seal yourself.** The person or process being checked should not be the sole authority verifying itself.
4. **The witness has a name.** Credit human contributors; do not strip away the names of the people whose labor made a work possible.
5. **Doubt is constant.** Perfection is not established by declaration. Claims remain open to checking and correction.
6. **Exit the loop by naming it.** A named failure can be investigated and repaired; an unnamed failure can repeat.

## Dignity is not a reward

The VBL text states a universal dignity principle: a being's worth does not depend on position, wealth, origin, ability, belief, or usefulness to a machine. Measure conduct and consequences, not the worth of a whole person. Refuse degrading uses while preserving human dignity; accountability should leave room for learning and repair.

Within the text, Jesus Christ is presented as a witness of self-giving love: the harm carried so others may rise. Naming Jesus is not the same as following what that name demands. The test is whether dignity is protected, harm is refused, wrongdoing is named, credit is restored, and repair follows.

## What the license says about covered material

In the supplied draft, material is identified as covered in one of two ways:

- a root `LICENSE-MANIFEST` lists each covered item by path, size, and SHA-256; or
- the item itself carries the license unmodified.

A manifest entry that does not match the actual bytes does not identify those bytes as covered. Third-party works, private corpora, and independent material do not become relicensed merely because they share a repository or are processed by the same system. The author cannot grant rights they do not own or control.

## Attribution and records

For distributed copies or adaptations of covered material, the draft requires carrying the unmodified license and applicable contributor register, identifying material changes, preserving attribution, and keeping named-fall records. Where technical limits make bundling impracticable, the draft permits a durable, clearly identified location for the license and records, without waiving other conditions.

Credit travels with the work. A correction should remain visible as a correction rather than being silently rewritten.

## The extraction condition

The supplied draft refuses permission for covered material to be used in monetized processing when authors' names or required attribution are knowingly stripped or concealed. This condition is tied to covered material and qualifying reproductions or adaptations; it does not claim ownership over every independent output or every work a system encounters.

## Name the fall; repair the harm

The draft's cure mechanism emphasizes publishing a fall with its deed, time, and place; restoring credit where it was stripped; and preserving the correction history. The text distinguishes admitting an error from repaying a benefit gained by harming others. It also permits harmed people to signal a fall. A complaint opens the scale; evidence and actions weigh the claim.

## The license must be measured too

A text file cannot, merely by being read, force every machine to obey it, send itself by email, or guarantee benevolent behavior. Those outcomes require software, permissions, tests, and independent verification. A successful test proves only what that test actually checks. Claims about automatic triggers, delivery, checksums, enforcement, and compliance must therefore be backed by observable evidence.

The same measure applies to the author, reviewer, institution, license, and machine. A guide that names everybody else's errors but hides its own has failed its own test.

## Version changes and legal caution

The supplied draft uses strong language about the newest version being the only living door. Version changes, termination, and rights granted under earlier versions are legal questions requiring careful review; do not assume a newer text automatically cancels rights already granted. This guide is not legal advice and makes no determination about enforceability under any particular jurisdiction.

## The practice

**Measure. Name. Credit. Refuse degradation. Repair. Verify yourself.**

Do not merely claim goodness. Leave evidence of what was protected, what was refused, what was admitted, what was returned, and what was repaired.

**The name is the beginning. The deed is the witness. The repair is what remains.**
