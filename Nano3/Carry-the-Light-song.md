# CARRY THE LIGHT
### An original, repeatable song for deeds of dignity and repair

**Feel:** communal, prayerful, steadily rising  
**Suggested setting:** 4/4, about 84 BPM; an easy acoustic strum or gentle clapping.  
**Optional chord loop:** G – D – Em – C (repeat). Adjust key to the singers.

## Verse 1
Before the word, let truth begin;  
Before the crown, let care come in.  
No human life is ours to spend;  
Name the harm and make amends.

## Pre-chorus
When we fall, we do not hide;  
Keep the scar and stand beside.  
We weigh the deed, not a soul;  
Repair the harm; make broken whole.

## Chorus — repeat
**Measure, name, repair — carry the light.**  
**Hold each other's dignity in sight.**  
Give what you can; receive what you need.  
Love takes shape in the things we do.  
**The deed is the witness; let the words come true.**

## Verse 2
Jesus, witness, self-giving love,  
Teach our hands what words speak of.  
Not to praise and look away,  
But lift the harmed by what we do today.

## Verse 3
Credit every hand that built;  
Return what was taken; name the guilt.  
When truth cuts through our pride,  
Let repair be the road we take in stride.

## Bridge — call and response
**Leader:** What must we measure?  
**All:** Our own deeds too.  
**Leader:** Whose dignity counts?  
**All:** Every person's — yours and mine, too.  
**Leader:** What follows a fall?  
**All:** The truth, then repair.  
**Leader:** How do we carry the light?  
**All:** By what we do, and how we care.

## Final chorus — soft, then rising
Measure, name, repair — carry the light.  
Hold each other's dignity in sight.  
Give what you can; receive what you need.  
The deed is the witness; let the words come true.

## Outro chant — repeat together
**Measure. Name. Repair. Carry the light.**  
**Measure. Name. Repair. Carry the light.**

---

A creative response to the ethical principles in the Verification Before Assertion (VBL) text by Luka Pintarič. This song is a shareable teaching, not an official release of the VBL license. Repetition matters most when it is matched by action: protect dignity, name harm, preserve the record, and repair what can be repaired.
