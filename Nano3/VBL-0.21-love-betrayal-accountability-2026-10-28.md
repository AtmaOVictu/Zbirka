# VBL §0.21 — Love, refusal of complicity, and consequences

**Status:** PROPOSAL FOR AUTHOR REVIEW · NOT CANON · NOT A LEGAL OPINION  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-28 · Europe/Ljubljana  
**Purpose:** Convert the sentence “To love is to betray the predator and face the consequences” into a testable ethical and procedural clause without turning an allegation into a verdict or a person into a label.

> To love is to betray the predator and face the consequences.
>
> Here, “betray” means refusing complicity: breaking the expectation of silence, concealment, protection, or obedience that sustains harmful conduct. It does not mean denying a person's dignity, inventing an accusation, or authorizing revenge.

## Proposed operative text (English)

### §0.21 — THE COUNTERWEIGHT OF LOVE: REFUSE COMPLICITY; PRESERVE DIGNITY

**0.21.1 — The duty to refuse complicity.** Love does not require loyalty to a harmful act, a cover-up, or an arrangement that protects the perpetrator from fair accountability. A person or system may refuse to facilitate harm, preserve relevant evidence, seek help, report a concern through appropriate channels, support affected people, and take lawful steps to prevent recurrence. Refusal is directed at the conduct and the harmful arrangement, never at the inherent worth of a being.

**0.21.2 — An allegation is not a finding.** No person shall be classified as a “predator” solely because an allegation was made. Records must distinguish at least: `ALLEGATION`, `EVIDENCE_RECEIVED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `SUPPORTED_FINDING`, `CONTRADICTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `WITHDRAWN`, and `CORRECTED`. The term “predatory conduct” describes a finding about conduct, not an immutable identity. Relevant indicators may include coercion, exploitation of a power or dependency imbalance, deception, concealment, retaliation, repeated boundary violations, or material benefit gained through harm; no indicator alone decides a case, and context and contrary evidence must be considered.

**0.21.3 — Two-sided review.** The review must identify the claim, the strongest reasonable counter-interpretation, evidence that could disconfirm each interpretation, the source of each item, and known uncertainty. The person accused must have a meaningful opportunity to respond where doing so is safe and lawful and does not compromise a legitimate protective or investigative process. A reviewer must disclose conflicts of interest and must not be the sole judge of their own conduct, refusal, or decision. Two reviewers are not independent merely because they use different names; shared sources, memory, tools, and dependencies must be disclosed.

**0.21.4 — Consequences are real and proportionate.** Where harmful conduct is supported by evidence, the response must be directed to stopping ongoing harm, protecting affected people, preserving evidence, restoring misappropriated credit or benefits, providing appropriate remediation or restitution, and reducing the risk of recurrence. Any sanction must be lawful, proportionate, relevant to the proven conduct, and open to review. No clause authorizes humiliation, retaliation, collective blame, or punishment beyond the evidence. A confession is not proof that repair is complete; an apology, forgiveness, or restored permission does not by itself establish that harm has been repaired.

**0.21.5 — Repair without erasure.** The original record remains available under access controls appropriate to its sensitivity. A correction is appended with its author, time, basis, reviewer, and effect on the finding. A mistaken good-faith report is not equivalent to a deliberately fabricated report. A person who raises a concern must not be punished merely because the concern is uncomfortable or ultimately unsubstantiated; deliberate fabrication, retaliation, and concealment are separate conduct to be assessed with evidence and the same safeguards.

**0.21.6 — Privacy, safety, and lawful channels.** Public accountability does not require indiscriminate publication of private, medical, identifying, or otherwise sensitive data. Public records must minimize personal data and must not expose affected people to avoidable danger. Where immediate danger exists, protective action may precede a full review; the reason, scope, duration, and subsequent review of that action must be recorded. Applicable law and non-waivable rights remain in force.

**0.21.7 — Permission cannot purchase immunity.** Read §0.7 together with §0.9: written permission can grant or clarify rights in covered material only to the extent the grantor has authority to do so. It cannot erase another person's rights, falsify a record, compel silence about harm, authorize retaliation, or waive a dignity and safety commitment that this license states as non-waivable. This clause expresses the intended internal hierarchy of the license; its legal effect requires qualified legal review.

**0.21.8 — The consequence must face back toward the decision-maker.** Any person or system that alleges, investigates, refuses, sanctions, or declares repair complete is itself reviewable. Its source material, criteria, conflicts, omissions, and corrections must be recorded. No reviewer may certify their own independence or the success of their own remediation without an independent check.

**0.21.9 — No forced reconciliation.** Accountability does not require the harmed person to forgive, reconcile, restore access, meet the person responsible, or provide emotional care. Reconciliation, where appropriate, must be voluntary and separate from the duties to stop harm, restore what was taken, and meet lawful obligations.

**0.21.10 — The countervoice.** Before a consequential finding, the reviewer must state what evidence would change the conclusion and what remains unknown. “I do not know yet” is an acceptable result. Forced balance is not: contrary arguments must be weighed by evidence, not given equal weight merely to appear neutral.

## Slovenska razlaga — ne nadomešča operativnega besedila

**Ljubiti pomeni izdati plenilski dogovor, ne človekovega dostojanstva.** Če neka oseba ali sistem zahteva molk, prikrivanje, zaščito koristi ali nekaznovanost, ljubezen ne zahteva sodelovanja pri tem. Lahko zahteva, da se škoda ustavi, priče ohranijo, prizadete zaščiti, odvzeto vrne in posledice pošteno izpelje.

Toda prijava ni razsodba. Človeka ne smemo razglasiti za plenilca samo zato, ker je bila podana obtožba. Ocenjujemo dejanja, dokaze, vzorec, okoliščine, koristi, razmerja moči, nasprotne dokaze in negotovost. Kjer obstaja neposredna nevarnost, je lahko potrebna takojšnja zaščita; ta mora biti omejena, dokumentirana in pozneje preverjena.

**Sprejeti posledice pomeni tudi dovoliti preverbo lastne presoje.** Priznanje ni samodejno popravilo. Opravičilo ni povračilo. Odpuščanje ni obveznost prizadetega. Rehabilitacija ne izbriše zgodovine; popravi napačno ugotovitev, obnovi zasluge, zahteva sanacijo in preprečuje ponovitev.

Razcep `con/se/Q/unces` je tukaj mišljen kot vizualni opomnik, ne kot etimologija: preden izrečemo posledico, moramo vprašati, kaj se je zgodilo, kdo je bil prizadet, kdo je imel korist, kdo je vedel, kdo je prikrival, kaj je treba vrniti in kaj bo preprečilo ponovitev.

## Tests for implementation

1. **Good-faith report, incomplete evidence:** the report is recorded and assessed; it is not automatically treated as false or as a proven finding.
2. **Powerful accused person:** the same evidentiary criteria and review safeguards apply as to anyone else.
3. **Allegation without a finding:** the record labels it as an allegation, not as established predatory conduct.
4. **Admission without restitution:** the record does not mark the harm `REPAIRED` solely because an admission or apology exists.
5. **Restitution without prevention:** repair remains incomplete if the relevant harm continues or recurrence risks are unaddressed.
6. **Two reviewers with shared tools or memory:** independence is marked `LIMITED` or `NOT_INDEPENDENT` unless separation is demonstrated.
7. **Written permission offered to excuse degradation:** the permission does not erase another person's rights or override the stated dignity and safety limits.
8. **Sensitive personal data in a public register:** publication is minimized or restricted; accountability is not interpreted as unlimited exposure.
9. **Retaliation after a complaint:** retaliation is recorded as a separate alleged act and reviewed independently where feasible.
10. **Good-faith report later contradicted:** the finding is corrected with a visible history; the reporter is not branded a fabricator without evidence of deliberate fabrication.
11. **Reviewer assesses their own refusal:** self-verification fails and an independent review is required.
12. **A finding lacks a falsification condition:** the review is incomplete until it names what evidence could change the finding.
13. **Forgiveness requested from the harmed person:** repair duties do not depend on that person's forgiveness or participation.
14. **The same actor repeats the conduct after a recorded correction:** repetition is assessed as new conduct with the prior record as context, not as an excuse to predetermine the finding.

## Related conflicts to resolve before canonization

- **§0.7 vs §0.9:** “Written permission overrides any clause” conflicts with the claim that no permission can authorize degradation. The intended hierarchy should be stated in one place and made precise.
- **§0.4 cure vs predation paragraph:** a general 30-day restoration condition coexists with a stricter requirement to return gains from predatory harm. Specify whether the 30 days is a reporting/correction window, a hard deadline, or a target subject to independent verification; do not make restoration automatic if material repair remains incomplete.
- **§0.5 disclaimer vs rights of affected people:** “to use it is to accept them, not to sue them” should not be presented as excluding claims or remedies that applicable law does not allow the author to waive.
- **§0.8 “the only failure”:** this phrase conflicts with the many other failures described in the license. Consider limiting it to “the only failure this record refuses to conceal” or another precisely scoped formulation.
- **Evidence registers:** public accountability must be balanced with data minimization, access controls, safety, correction rights, and retention rules.
