A scope manifest should classify each relevant asset as included, excluded, or unresolved, with a reason and rights basis. An unresolved asset must not silently become licensed.****## 02****Separate historical agreements from new grants.****§§8 and 10 declare the newest version the only living door and deny continuing force to earlier grants. That intention needs careful legal review. A new publication cannot, merely by declaring itself superior, establish that earlier permissions or accrued rights have vanished. Record the exact version and terms applicable to each grant.****## 03****Turn the ledger into an auditable event history.****KORENINA says corrections keep their scars. Implement this with append-only correction events, stable identifiers, evidence references, timestamps, and explicit state transitions. Preserve the difference between a reported fall, a substantiated finding, a disputed allegation, an applied cure, and independent verification. Append-only storage alone is not proof against tampering.****## 04****Make independent verification reproducible.****The “two hands” rule should be a release gate, not just a principle. Publish verification scripts, dependencies, test fixtures, expected outputs, and a procedure for an independent reviewer to rerun. The verifier should assess the exact candidate artifact, identified by digest.****## 05****Distinguish an ethical demand from a technical guarantee.****§12's byte-identity claim can be tested for specified artifacts and transmission paths. Its claim that reading the file switches on every machine, forces an upgrade each session, or prevents a carrier from claiming ignorance cannot be guaranteed by prose alone. Specify an actual mechanism, its scope, and its failure modes—or explicitly retain the passage as normative language.****## 06****Protect the harmed without turning allegations into verdicts.****§4 gives affected people standing to name a fall. Preserve that access, but also define evidence handling, privacy, response rights, correction, appeal, and publication thresholds. A person's ability to report harm should not depend on the author's presence; an allegation should not automatically become a substantiated finding.****## The deeper issue in §9****The most important distinction in the entire text may be this:****> The refusal reads the deed, never the being.****That principle gives the rest of VBL a coherent direction. The system should evaluate conduct and evidence without assigning different inherent worth to people. It should be capable of refusing a harmful request while retaining a route to correction, accountability, and restoration.****But the operational question is still open: who determines whether a use degrades dignity, what evidence is sufficient, and how is disagreement handled? VBL's own epistemic discipline requires those decisions to be documented rather than hidden inside an assertion that a machine “knows the measure.”****The Christian witness, forgiveness, and love should remain recognizable as the moral foundation chosen by the author. They should not be represented as a technical property that a machine has acquired simply by reading the file.****## One immediate textual defect****The supplied material is not a clean canonical artifact.
