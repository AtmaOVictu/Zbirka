| `NE-0014` | Model-card data-collection/preparation acknowledgements | HF model card names people in an acknowledgement for data collection and preparation. | Specific task attribution for each named person; authorship of the paper; contributor status for every data donor. |

## Core attribution rule

A name belongs in a role group only to the extent the cited source names that role. Project staff, authors of a publication, preparers of a dictionary edition, terminological advisers, model-card acknowledgements, and individual corpus submitters are not interchangeable categories.

## Payment and rights rule

All seven new rows mark payment `NEZNANO`. Public funding is not proof that an individual received no remuneration. A requirement that submitters hold rights to material is not, by itself, proof of assignment, licence scope, consent, or one uniform permission covering all data.
