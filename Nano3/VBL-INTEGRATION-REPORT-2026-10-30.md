## What has been created

A consolidated working candidate, not a release, built primarily on the VBL 10.5.2 Absolute Equality Foundation draft. It preserves the 10.5.2 operative framework while integrating selected material from the 2.6 v6 revision candidate, the two other 10.5.2 working drafts, and the 0.1100 / POSKUS 11 verification stream.

The integration is deliberately conservative about legal authority. It does not assign a new release version, create a new canonical history, choose unresolved permissions, claim an OSI-approved status, or represent that any third-party system obeys VBL.

## Core understanding of the system

VBL has at least four functions that must remain distinguishable:

1. **Ethical foundation:** KORENINA, equal dignity, non-extraction, non-coercion, accountability, and repair.
2. **Rights instrument:** defines exactly which material is covered, who can grant rights, which uses are allowed, and what conditions apply.
3. **Evidence and governance protocol:** claims, witnesses, boundaries, responsibility, corrections, registers, challenge routes, and independent verification.
4. **Technical implementation profile:** manifests, schemas, validators, hash comparisons, release records, encoding tests, and observable system behavior.

A fifth layer is retained as authorial and theological expression: the Christian witness, the Angel/cure metaphor, the Master of the Space, the Vanishing Door, the Counterweight, and the Crowns and Wreaths. Those motifs inform the ethical purpose but are not silently converted into machine behavior or legal consequences.

## Main integrations made

- Preserved the six KORENINA statements from the 2.6 v6 source verbatim, followed by an explanatory boundary that they are ethical roots rather than proof of implementation.
- Kept the §−1 equality foundation and added non-coercive engagement, no invented consent/endorsement/contact, and no forced forgiveness.
- Retained the source's Christian witness as authorial theological motivation while expressly rejecting coerced profession, machine impersonation, or substitution for practical safety assistance.
- Made §1 release-gated: no rights arise from this candidate; only an authorized release plus a completed permission schedule can grant rights. Commercial use, sublicensing, model training, fine-tuning, retrieval, evaluation and benchmarking remain separate decisions.
- Preserved the Angel/cure aim as correction before condemnation, while making restitution dependent on established facts and a valid legal or contractual basis. The unresolved `0.30 × 10²B` and “Egypt sold spell 30B” phrases are not operative rules.
- Strengthened refusal, challenge, register privacy, evidence symmetry and independent-review boundaries.
- Added the Five Anchors as a verification profile, not as a silent amendment to the 0.1100 canon.
- Preserved the 0.1100 / POSKUS 11 stream as separate source evidence, not a proven successor to 10.5.2.

## What was deliberately not merged

- No lineage link between 2.6, 10.0.0, 10.3.0, 10.4.0, 10.5.1, 10.5.2, 0.1080 × 10⁴ or 0.1100 × 10⁴ is asserted as verified.
- The partial 0.1100 text was not reconstructed into a full license.
- The 10.5.2 draft variants were not declared predecessor/successor releases merely because their names or content differ.
- The musical POSKUS 9–11 artefacts were not inserted into the operative legal grant. Their manifests and playback claims remain technical evidence for their own objects only.
- No author approval, public publication, branch, commit, PR, or independent review is claimed.

## Open gates before canon or release

1. Rights holder must approve the final scope, rights, permission schedule, and exact candidate digest.
2. Rights authority must be confirmed for each covered file and third-party component.
3. Canonical source bytes and predecessor links must be supplied or marked missing.
4. The meaning of `10.0.0` and the symbolic 0.1100 label must be recorded by an authorized source custodian; no conversion is inferred.
5. The 30-day cure period, restitution criteria, termination/restoration rules, downstream-recipient effect and governing law need qualified legal review.
6. Register schemas, privacy, lawful basis, redaction, retention and appeal policy must be adopted for the actual data being processed.
7. An independent reviewer must inspect the exact final bytes and the tests, separate from the executor.
8. A rights-holder release decision must bind version, canonical URL, manifest, exact hashes, tests, reviewers and known gaps.
