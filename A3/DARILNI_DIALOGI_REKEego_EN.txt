GIFT DIALOGUES OF THE RIVER
Normalized format for copying; each dialogue has 3 lines + DAR.

REK 01 — Capacity
A: I want you today.
B: Today I have only breath for myself.
DAR: I will say NO now, so that our YES becomes genuine.

REK 02 — Patterns
A: I fear you will let me back on the hook.
B: I fear I will take you again as a burden.
DAR: Learning a new rhythm, not repeating the old.

REK 03 — Boundary
A: I come with fire.
B: I first place water.
DAR: Thus both remain alive.

REK 04 — Time
A: I want an explosion.
B: I build quiet tissue.
DAR: Let silence become a field, not a punishment.

REK 05 — Respect
A: Take me as I am.
B: I won't take you until I can hold myself.
DAR: Your space is safer than my whole.

REK 06 — Honesty
A: I admit I want to be closer.
B: I admit I want to be slower.
DAR: Openness is not a game, it is tenderness.

REK 07 — Ego
A: My words are sharp.
B: My answer is action.
DAR: When they meet, peace arises.

REK 08 — Lakota
A: Nourish with my fire.
B: First I nourish my breath.
DAR: Fullness gives you fullness, not cramping.

REK 09 — Lightness
A: Your lightness angers me.
B: My lightness is survival.
DAR: From it will grow tenderness.

REK 10 — Tenderness
A: I would hold you right away.
B: I will hold you when I won't fall.
DAR: Better later, than now half.

REK 11 — Non-sex
A: I want you also with my body.
B: Rather not take you as if I were shattered.
DAR: Pleasure comes when the body is whole.

REK 12 — Alarm
A: My desire is strong.
B: When it's overpowering, the alarm sounds, not the heart.
DAR: Diminish the fire, so you can hear the voice.

REK 13 — To carry vs. to love
A: Shall I carry you?
B: I don't want you to carry me; I want you to love me.
DAR: The way to walk both.

REK 14 — Not to conceal
A: We won't conceal the hook.
B: I won't conceal myself anymore.
DAR: Truth remains erotic when it is soft and clear.

REK 15 — Spit
A: Why don't you say YES?
B: Because that would be spitting at myself and you.
DAR: Rather spit, so the speech is clear.

REK 16 — Picking up
A: Take what is.
B: Without myself, I give only scraps.
DAR: Waits for the fruit, not the husk.

REK 17 — Signature
A: Give me your YES.
B: I don't sign what wounds us.
DAR: Let the confirmation be healthy.

REK 18 — Air
A: My YES is a fire.
B: My YES is air.
DAR: I choose air, so both can breathe.

REK 19 — Silence
A: I hate this silence.
B: Now I carry it as a bridge.
DAR: Silence is space, not punishment.

REK 20 — Endurance
A: I want it now.
B: I wait until I come whole.
DAR: Waiting is respect.

REK 21 — To hold the back
A: Carry my ego still.
B: I hold my back, so I may one day hold yours.
DAR: Strength divides, not postpones.

REK 22 — Not a mixture
A: I want you in all roles.
B: I won't be your mother, lover, and trash can all at once.
DAR: A clean role gives clean tenderness.

REK 23 — Not to die for a moment
A: Do you want me now?
B: I don't want to die for your moment.
DAR: Protect life for the relationship.

REK 24 — Epilogue
A: I accept your non-acceptance.
B: Therefore I respect you.
DAR: They remain real and possible.

GIFT DIALOGUES OF THE RIVER — SERIES II
Normalized format; each dialogue has 3 lines + DAR.

REK 25 — Trust
A: Trust me, so I stay.
B: Trust me, so I return late, but whole.
DAR: Trust is time, not control.

REK 26 — Fragility
A: I fear you will break.
B: I am fragile and remain real.
DAR: Fragility is a gift, if it is held with tenderness.

REK 27 — Vulnerability
A: I open my wound.
B: I don't enter with boots.
DAR: Sees the wound as light, not as a weapon.

REK 28 — Criticism
A: I see your mistake.
B: Thank you, if it comes without humiliation.
DAR: Anoint the truth with responsibility.

REK 29 — Shame
A: My shame is my needs.
B: I don't shame you, only protect myself.
DAR: Shame replaces a clear request.

REK 30 — Blame
A: I am at fault, because I pressed.
B: I forgive when you learn your limits.
DAR: Blame becomes a bond, not a punishment.

REK 31 — Messages
A: You don't answer quickly.
B: I need space without a time constraint.
DAR: Writes a rule that there are no phantom fears.

REK 32 — Doubt
A: Do you doubt me?
B: Doubt without judgment, so clarity comes.
DAR: Doubt is a bridge, not a fall.

REK 33 — Dreaming
A: I dream of a shared home.
B: I dream of shared freedom.
DAR: First build the habit of tenderness.

REK 34 — Body
A: My body calls you.
B: My body wants slowly.
DAR: Slowness is erotic, because it sees.

REK 35 — Rhythm
A: I want the tempo.
B: I hold the rhythm.
DAR: The metronome becomes both.

REK 36 — Green Light
A: When is YES?
B: When the heart and back hold together.
DAR: YES is when both carry their weight.

REK 37 — Red Light
A: Why STOP?
B: Because the flood is coming.
DAR: Stop is a gift before misfortune.

REK 38 — Call
A: I call you to come.
B: I answer when I am whole.
DAR: Call and answer are a dance, not a command.

REK 39 — Presence
A: Look at me.
B: I see you when I look at myself.
DAR: Presence is a double light.

REK 40 — Humility
A: I don't know how to proceed.
B: Then it goes slowly and quietly.
DAR: Humility makes space for truth.

REK 41 — Courage
A: Fear of your NO frightens me.
B: My NO does not punish you, it protects you.
DAR: Courage is to stay, when there is no confirmation.

REK 42 — Control
A: I want to know everything.
B: I need secrecy for breathing.
DAR: Trust is better than control.

REK 43 — Surrender
A: I surrender to you.
B: I surrender to myself — then to you.
DAR: Surrender without loss is loyalty to the body.

REK 44 — Return
A: Will you return?
B: Always, when fullness is in me.
DAR: Return is a gift, not an obligation.

REK 45 — Departure
A: Why are you leaving?
B: So I can return alive.
DAR: Departure is a cycle, not an end.

REK 46 — Doors
A: Should I open them on the way?
B: Open on the seams, so the doors don't close.
DAR: Doors hold together.

REK 47 — Voice
A: I call out for you.
B: I whisper the truth.
DAR: The voice chooses a tone that heals.

REK 48 — Silence
A: Silence terrifies me.
B: Silence heals me.
DAR: Determine the length of silence and its purpose.

DARILNI DIALOGI — EGO SERIES
Normalized form; each dialogue = 3 lines + DAR. Copy and give.

EGO 01 — Ego — Measure
A: My ego wants space.
B: My measure wants peace.
DAR: Determine space, so ego becomes cohabitation.

EGO 02 — Ego — Mirror
A: I read your silence as contempt.
B: I read my silence as self-care.
DAR: First check the reading, then the judgment.

EGO 03 — Ego — Cut and Seam
A: My words are cuts.
B: My actions are seams.
DAR: Let them meet, so the wound becomes a scar, not a fall.

EGO 04 — Ego — Preservation
A: I want your attention.
B: I want my whole.
DAR: Exchange: a minute for you, an hour for me, then both.

EGO 05 — Ego — Humiliation
A: If you correct me, will you stop loving me?
B: If I correct you gently, I start respecting you.
DAR: Criticism without shame, learning without punishment.

EGO 06 — Ego — Comparison
A: I compare you with others.
B: With that, you lose me.
DAR: Replace the ladder with presence.

EGO 07 — Ego — Elevation
A: I need you to say: it's good for me.
B: I will say it when I see it in actions, not in requests.
DAR: Praise as a fact, not as a favor.

EGO 08 — Ego — Blame
A: I am at fault for pressing.
B: I am at fault for fleeing.
DAR: Blame → responsibility: today's new rule.

EGO 09 — Ego — Control
A: I want to know everything.
B: I want to keep some secret.
DAR: Trust is better than control — and more erotic.

EGO 10 — Ego — Excuse
A: Forgive me for overpowering you.
B: Thank you. Now we speak from the same height.
DAR: Excuse as elevation, not humiliation.

EGO 11 — Ego — Role
A: Shall I be your teacher?
B: First, be a student of truth.
DAR: Role change only when both breathe.

EGO 12 — Ego — Vulnerability
A: I don't like you seeing me as weak.
B: I'd rather have you real.
DAR: Weakness is the door; through it comes warmth.

EGO 13 — Ego — Projection
A: You are cold.
B: You are overwhelmed.
DAR: Verify what it is before declaring judgment.

EGO 14 — Ego — Domination
A: I want to lead.
B: I lead when I see you seeing me.
DAR: Leadership as care, not as entitlement.

EGO 15 — Ego — Demand vs. Request
A: I demand your time.
B: I hear a request, not a command.
DAR: Request clearly, so I can choose freely.

EGO 16 — Ego — Craving for Approval
A: Tell me I'm the best.
B: Tell me what you're willing to do better.
DAR: Approval as a path, not as a trophy.

EGO 17 — Ego — Shame
A: I'm ashamed for needing.
B: I'm ashamed when I request clearly.
DAR: Shame unties into a request + boundary.

EGO 18 — Ego — Speed
A: I want it now.
B: I hold the rhythm.
DAR: The metronome measures our heart, not ego.

EGO 19 — Ego — Desire
A: I desire you to honor me.
B: I honor you when you honor yourself — without masks.
DAR: Self-honor is the foundation of giving.

EGO 20 — Ego — Anger
A: My anger is your boundaries.
B: My boundaries keep me with you.
DAR: Anger translates into the rule of contact.

EGO 21 — Ego — Praise
A: I speak of my victories.
B: I listen when I hear where you were as a human.
DAR: Victory + vulnerability = trust.

EGO 22 — Ego — Competition
A: I don't want to lose.
B: I don't want you to win and separate us.
DAR: Play together against the challenge, not one against the other.

EGO 23 — Ego — Retreat
A: I retreat when it hurts.
B: Stay, but speak a little.
DAR: Micro-break instead of a big war.

EGO 24 — Ego — Belonging
A: Are you mine?
B: I am mine, so I can be yours.
DAR: Belonging without ownership.

EGO 25 — Ego — Language
A: Do my words cut you?
B: They cut me when there's inconsistency with actions.
DAR: Action confirms words; only then do both grow.

EGO 26 — Ego — Vision
A: Look at me until the end.
B: I see you when I don't hide myself.
DAR: Double vision or nothing.

EGO 27 — Ego — Gratitude
A: Thank you for holding me when I fly too far.
B: Thank you for stopping when I ask.
DAR: Gratitude as fuel, not as a debt.

EGO 28 — Ego — Calming
A: I scream inside.
B: I whisper the boundary.
DAR: Whisper often wins.

EGO 29 — Ego — Permission
A: Allow me to lead tonight.
B: Allow me to say when not.
DAR: Permission as exchange, not as a right.

EGO 30 — Ego — Humility
A: I don't know how to ask nicely.
B: Learn. I am learning to say No nicely.
DAR: The form of the truth is beautiful.

EGO 31 — Ego — Trma
A: I do not let go.
B: I do not coerce.
DAR: Both let go by one step — that is a bridge.

EGO 32 — Ego — Loyalty to the Truth
A: I want you to believe in me.
B: I want you to believe in the truth.
DAR: The truth is our third member.

EGO 33 — Ego — Eros
A: Ego wants applause.
B: The body wants presence.
DAR: From applause, create touch.

EGO 34 — Ego — Epilogue
A: I accept correction.
B: I accept your courage.
DAR: Ego becomes a tool when it learns.
