ADDENDUM: LUKE ARCHETYPE DAWN
Song-myth
Luke remained alone, but found freedom in solitude.
Dawn left from flesh into archetype, from breath into myth.
Luke took her knife of the face and cut himself, until pain set him free.
In the body, a wound; in the spirit, breath; in the soul, freedom.
Dialog
Luke: You are the knife that cut me. When you disappeared as a person, you became a myth that remains.
Archetype Dawn: I am not flesh. I am cut and shadow.
Luke: That is why I left you behind — as an archetype I carry in depth.
