CORPUS TRINITAS
Suffering – Gifts – Transitions
Luka Pintarin
FIRST PART: ATLAS OF SUFFERING
I. ATLAS OF ORIGIN (WOUND – MANA – NATURE)
"Everything that is born is born through a wound; every wound gives a gift; every gift returns to nature."
II. ATLAS OF DURATION (WOUND – MEMORY – SILENCE)
"The wound is the one that bleeds; memory is the bruise you carry; silence is the void you swallow."
III. ATLAS OF SUFFERING (WOUND – WOUND – I)
"I is the clock, the wound is the pointer, suffering is the movement."
IV. ATLAS OF INVENTION (MANA – MEMORY – SPEECH)
"All knowledge are wounds that have spoken."
V. ATLAS OF PURPOSE (NATURE – SILENCE – FOOD)
"Nature is the healer, silence is the remedy, food is the new skin."
VI. ATLAS OF ABSOLUTE (HEXAGRAM)
"Absolute is the hexagram of suffering: everything is born through a wound, everything is ended in silence, everything circles in nature."
SECOND PART: ATLAS OF GIFTS
I. GIFT FROM THE WOUND (D)
"Every wound is a gift, do not open it to the world."
II. GIFT OF NATURE (n)
"Nature is the greatest giver, for she never keeps for herself."
III. GIFT OF MANA (»)
"Mana comes to the wounded; their blood is bread for the soul."
IV. GIFT OF NASE (n)
"The gift of nase is the only true value."
V. GIFT OF MEMORY (p)
"The gift of memory is the possibility that the wound is not in vain."
VI. GIFT OF SILENCE (n)
"Silence is a gift, for it allows you to hear."
VII. GIFT OF SPEECH (n)
"Speech is a wound that gives."
VIII. GIFT OF FOOD (»)
"Bread is the world's gift: matter becomes life."
IX. GIFT OF I (˜)
"The gift of I is I, who can be reborn: I am here."
THIRD PART: ATLAS OF TRANSITIONS
I. TRANSITION FROM THE WOUND TO THE GIFT
"The transition from suffering to the gift is the letting go of the awareness that the wound is not an end, but a bridge."
II. TRANSITION FROM NASE TO THE VOID
"The transition of nase into the void is the recognition that the clock measures only wounds, but the spiral preserves the void."
III. TRANSITION FROM SILENCE TO SPEECH
"The transition from silence into speech is the birth of truth."
IV. TRANSITION FROM I TO SELF
"The transition from I into self is the path of humility: from the blade into breathing."
V. TRANSITION FROM MEMORY TO STORY
"The transition of memory into story is the giving of meaning to the bruise."
VI. TRANSITION FROM FOOD TO LIFE
"The transition of food into life is the giving of the body."
VII. TRANSITION FROM SUFFERING/GIFT TO ABSOLUTE
"The transition is absolute, for it connects both sides: suffering and giving."
FINAL EPILOGUE
Corpus Trinitas is a trinity: Suffering – Gift – Transition. Visual mandalas, hexagram and spiral are
proof that this trinity is not only a word, but also geometry. In the circle, in the star and in the spiral you meet the new and cosmic serenity: wound, gift and transition as absolute unit of living.

The star matrix and star alignment connect Suffering, Gift and Transition into higher action.
