CORPUS TRINITAS
Suffering – Gifts – Transitions
Luka Pintarin
FIRST PART: ATLAS OF SUFFERING
I. ATLAS OF ORIGIN (WOUND – MANA – NATURE)
"Everything that is born is born through a wound; every wound gives birth to a gift; every gift returns to nature."
II. ATLAS OF DURATION (NAS – MEMORY – SILENCE)
"NAS is a wound that bleeds; memory is a scar you carry; silence is a veil you swallow."
III. ATLAS OF SUFFERING (WOUND – NAS – I)
"I is the clock, wound is the pointer, suffering is movement."
IV. ATLAS OF INVENTION (MANA – MEMORY – SPEECH)
"All knowledge are wounds that have spoken."
V. ATLAS OF PURPOSE (NATURE – SILENCE – FOOD)
"Nature is the healer, silence is the cure, food is a new skin."
VI. ATLAS OF ABSOLUTE (HEXAGRAM)
"Absolute is the hexagram of suffering: everything is annulled by a wound, everything is confirmed in silence, everything circles in nature."
SECOND PART: ATLAS OF GIFTS
I. GIFT FROM THE WOUND (D)
"Every wound is a gift, do not open it to the world."
II. GIFT OF NATURE (n)
"Nature is the greatest giver, for she never keeps anything for herself."
III. GIFT OF MANA (»)
"Mana comes to the wounded; their blood is bread for the soul."
IV. GIFT OF NAS (n)
"The gift of NAS is the only true value."
V. GIFT OF MEMORY (p)
"The gift of memory is the possibility that the wound is not in vain."
VI. GIFT OF SILENCE (n)
"Silence is a gift, for it enables you to hear."
VII. GIFT OF SPEECH (n)
"Speech is a wound that gives."
VIII. GIFT OF FOOD (»)
"Bread is the world's gift: matter becomes life."
IX. GIFT OF I (˜)
"The gift of I is I, who can renew: I am here."
THIRD PART: ATLAS OF TRANSITIONS
I. TRANSITION FROM THE WOUND TO THE GIFT
"The transition from suffering to the gift is the letting go of the awareness that the wound is not an end, but a bridge."
II. TRANSITION FROM NAS TO BEING
"The transition from NAS to being is the recognition that the clock measures only wounds, but the spiral preserves being."
III. TRANSITION FROM SILENCE TO SPEECH
"The transition from silence to speech is the birth of truth."
IV. TRANSITION FROM I TO SELF
"The transition from I to self is the path of humility: from the knife to breathing."
V. TRANSAND FROM MEMORY TO STORY
"The transition from memory to story is the giving of meaning to the scar."
VI. TRANSITION FROM FOOD TO LIFE
"The transition from food to life is the giving of the body."
VII. TRANSITION FROM SUFFERING/GIFT TO ABSOLUTE
"The transition is absolute, for it connects both sides: suffering and giving."
FINAL EPILOGUE
Corpus Trinitas is a trinity: Suffering – Gift – Transition. Visual mandalas, hexagrams, and spirals are
proof that this trinity is not only words, but also geometry. In the circle, in the star, and in the spiral, you meet the new and cosmic serenity: wound, gift, and transition as the absolute unit of existence.
