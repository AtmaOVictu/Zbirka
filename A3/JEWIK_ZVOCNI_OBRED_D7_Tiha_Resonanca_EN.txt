JEWIK SOUND RITUAL D7 - SILENT RESONANCE
Liturgical Guide for Performers of the JEWIK Sound Initiation
Ritual Steps:
- 1. Prepare the space in silence (T).
- 2. Breathe according to the rhythm: 4s INHALE (SILENCE), 4s EXHALE (WORD).
- 3. Repeat 7 times: follow the transition through intermediate frequencies (WORD NO).
- 4. Activate AGENTA_VOKAL (V) so the sound shapes the essence.
- 5. At the 7th breath, activate AGENTA_TIINE (T) as a conclusion.
- 6. Speak through G without voice, with presence.
- 7. Conclude in the inner resonance.
Agent Roles:
V (AGENT_VOKAL): articulates the sound through the transition
T (AGENT_TIINE): holds the silence as the final sound
G (AGENT_TII_GOVOR): hidden agent of inner speech
