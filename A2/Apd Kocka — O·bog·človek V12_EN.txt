PERVERZIJA — sectioning and segmentation
Definition: Perversion = positive apparent growth (Δ_cell ≥ 0), where integrity is low and toxicity or mask is high, thus the growth direction is distorted.
• Integrity: I = 0.5·ρ_res + 0.3·ρ_odg + 0.2·ρ_prav
• Toxicity: T = 0.6·σ_poh + 0.3·σ_strah + 0.1·σ_apa
• Mask of speech: M = max(0, g(G)) − ρ_res (clip in [0,1])
• Perversion index: Π_per = clip(0.6·T + 0.4·M − I, 0, 1)
Classification: mild ≥ 0.30 · strong ≥ 0.60 · critical ≥ 0.80.
Diagnostic hooks (tripwires):
1. κ′ ≥ 0 and I < 0.40 and T ≥ 0.50 → perversion: toxic growth.
2. κ′ ≥ 0 and M ≥ 0.20 and ρ_res < 0.30 → perversion: masked speech.
3. Δ_cell oscillates with S > 0.10 at the same time T ≥ I → perversion: unstable explosion.
SECTIONING (4-cut protocol)
1. U (Weights): temporarily reduce negative masses: β_poh′ = β_poh·(1 − Π_per), β_strah′ = β_strah·(1 − 0.5·Π_per).
2. M (Mask): stop the effect of g(G)+ (set g(G)+ = 0), until ρ_res ≥ 0.40 and explicitly switch speech to ∆H⟡ (commitment).
3. T (Tok): disable positive δ_E (set δ_E ≤ 0), until I ≥ T (cleaning of credits).
4. K (Conversion): recalculate κ′ with constraints; if κ′ ≥ 0 and I < 0.50 ⇒ CONVERSION: stay in phase (Δ_cell := min(Δ_cell, 0.05)), until I ≥ 0.50.
Antinom of perversion: CONVERSION (cleaning) — transformation of growth energy into real growth: requires I ≥ T and M → 0.

• Conditions: κ′ ≥ 0.60, I ≥ 0.60, S ≤ 0.05 (S = deviation of the last three κ′).
