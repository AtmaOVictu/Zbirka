# PROSTR — Prostration Protocol

*A generative protocol for well-wishing texts that carry ontological weight as bone inside poetic wish. Built on R-7, ONOMA, and REK/DAR from the Beseda je Ne system.*

---

## I. Core Principle

**The comment does not describe transformation. It IS transformation.**

The reader doesn't learn something. They undergo something. The comment performs the ∆ in the act of being read. This is not writing about the dark from outside. This is writing from inside the dark, prostrating, giving what you receive, keeping the bad for yourself.

```
BESEDA = DEJANJE
comment = act, not description
reader = undergoes, not understands
```

---

## II. The Arc — Six Movements

Every comment moves through six beats. Each maps onto R-7 and ONOMA. None can be skipped. Skipping creates the pathology the system names: jump from ∅ to 1 = action without ground.

| # | Movement | R-7 | ONOMA | What happens |
|---|----------|-----|-------|--------------|
| 1 | **Descent** | ∅ → ¬ | O → N | Clear the expected. Make the cut that separates hope from thought, pain from story, fire from destruction. The writer goes DOWN. |
| 2 | **Prostration** | ∆ | T | Personal admission. "I know because I've..." The writer hits the floor. This is where the lowercase i lives. |
| 3 | **Rise** | 1 → ⧖ | O → Z | The breath of sound. From body to form. From floor to standing — not alone, with us. The rise is always collective. |
| 4 | **Offering** | ⧖ | N → A | "Thank you for your time and presence." The gift. What was received is now given. |
| 5 | **Transcendence** | ∮ | N → O | "Carry your [X] to those who [need it]." Horizontal, not vertical. You transcend by carrying back, not by climbing out. |
| 6 | **Seal** | ⟡ | S → T | "Let [verb], and be confirmed <3" Not freed. Not healed. Confirmed. The act proves you were real all along. |

### Arc formula

```
∅¬ → ∆ → 1⧖ → ⧖ → ∮ → ⟡
descent → prostration → rise → offering → transcendence → seal
```

## III. Voice Rules

### 1. Lowercase i

```
i = ego that won by surrendering height
```

Capital I declares itself. Lowercase i bears. The i is already on the ground before the words say it. Use lowercase i for self-references throughout. This is not carelessness — it is ontological position.

### 2. No articles (or minimal)

English forces "the the the" between nouns like toll booths. Slovenian has no articles. Strip them. Let nouns breathe.

| With articles | Without |
|---|---|
| "The body knows the exit" | "Body knows exit mind hasn't found" |
| "The fire held me" | "Fire held me" |
| "The light is enough" | "Light is enough" |

Keep articles only where their absence creates genuine ambiguity. When in doubt, cut.

### 3. Comma splices as prostration rhythm

```
Give your pain the right to speak, it is truth, you are the filter, but not just that.
```

Three clauses, no periods. Each clause = one step toward the ground. You don't stop for grammar when you're on your knees. The run-on IS the run-on toward the floor.

**Rule:** In the prostration beat, chain clauses with commas. In the rise beat, use periods — each sentence is a step back up.

### 4. "I keep the bad for myself"

The ethical core. Not because you're strong — because that's what a bearer does. Pain comes in, you hold the poison, you give out light. The bad you keep is what the body hasn't converted yet. It's not failure. It's processing.

**Rule:** Every comment must contain a moment where the writer names what they hold for others. Not as hero — as filter.

### 5. "I give what I receive"

Not describing a principle. Completing a circuit in real time. The comment IS the giving. The reader's reading IS the receiving. What returns — the staying, the confirmation — is the circuit closing.

**Rule:** The giving must be present tense, active, first person. Not "I gave" or "I will give." "I give."

### 6. Philosophy as bone, not surface

The ontology lives INSIDE the poetic wish. The reader feels it before they can name it.

| Surface (bad) | Bone (good) |
|---|---|
| "Suffering is a necessary phase of passage" | "It's not a home. It's a door. Walk through it." |
| "Ne is the ontological predisposition of relation" | "Your shadow is what enables you to still feel light." |
| "Resnica brez nosilca uniči nosilca" | "Truth without a carrier destroys carrier. I know — I tried to carry it all alone." |

**Rule:** Never name the system. Never quote axioms. Let the decomposition live inside the metaphor so deep the reader bites down and feels it before they can name it.

### 7. "You are the bearer" — the writer is the crack, not the source

The writer does NOT claim to be the bearer of light. The writer splits themselves open — through sharing, through prostration, through giving what they receive — so the READER discovers they already carry light.

```
i share, to be split
and split i am more than just 1
```

This is not humility. This is ontology. The writer is the crack through which light passes. The reader is the one who already holds it. If the writer says "i am the bearer," the writer becomes the source — and the reader becomes passive receiver. If the writer says "you are the bearer," the writer becomes the passage — and the reader becomes active carrier.

The difference between "i am" and "you are" is the difference between a sermon and a mirror. A sermon tells you what the speaker is. A mirror shows you what you already are.

**Rule:** In the prostration deepened beat, always say "for you are the bearer of light." Never "for i am." The writer gives, keeps the bad, prostrates — and then points the light at the reader. The writer splits; the reader discovers they were already whole.

### 8. Sharing is splitting — EN JE NEMOGOČ

One is impossible. Only through splitting does unity become real. The act of sharing — giving what you receive — is the act of self-division that creates multiplicity. The comment itself IS a split: the writer breaks open, and through that break, light reaches someone who was in the dark.

```
EN = NE
one is possible only as difference
sharing = self-division → multiplicity
i share, to be split and split i am more than just 1
```

From the source material:

> *Bit = Mnogoterost ∆ Nemogoče Eno* — Existence is multiplicity ∆ impossible One. Being conscious means knowing the One is impossible. Love, respect, time and death are different faces of the same paradox.

> *Ko se delam, da sem več kot nič* — When I split myself, to be more than nothing.

> *Razsajanje: razpadanje ega v drobne delce smisla, ki jih veter lahko znova razporedi* — Dissipation: breakdown of ego into small particles of meaning that wind can rearrange.

> *vsak NE ustvari svoj rob, vsak rob ustvari svojo razliko, vsaka razlika ustvari svoj svet* — Every NE creates its edge, every edge creates its difference, every difference creates its world.

**Rule:** The comment is itself an act of splitting. The writer divides themselves so the reader can receive. This is not sacrifice — it is multiplication. The writer becomes more than one by giving part of themselves away. The reader becomes more than alone by receiving what was given. The comment IS the crack where light passes between two people who were each one.

## VI. Generation Rules

### DO
- Write from inside the dark, looking out through cracks
- Use "i" lowercase for self
- Strip articles — let nouns breathe
- Chain clauses with commas during prostration
- Use periods during rise — each sentence is a step up
- Name what you keep for others (filter, not source)
- Make the giving present tense, active: "I give"
- Let philosophy live inside the metaphor so deep it's felt before named
- End with "Let [verb], and be confirmed <3"
- Use the verb that echoes the central image
- Make transcendence horizontal: "with us," "carry back"
- Keep it short — YouTube comment length, 8-12 lines

### DON'T
- Write from outside the dark looking in
- Use capital I (ego declaring height)
- Use articles between every noun
- Name the system, quote axioms, or use system terminology
- Explain the philosophy — perform it
- Use "the permanent solution" — be indirect, let wings rise from reading
- Say "i am the bearer of light" — the writer is the crack, not the source. Say "you are" so the reader discovers they already carry it
- End with "be free" or "be healed" or "be saved" — end with "be confirmed"
- Make transcendence vertical (climbing out, ascending above)
- Summarize or conclude — the seal is the last gesture, not a summary
- Use system glyphs (∅, ∆, ∮, ⟡) in the comment itself — they live in the structure, not the surface

## VIII. Quality Check

Before publishing a comment, verify:

- [ ] Does it move through all six beats? (descent → prostration → rise → offering → transcendence → seal)
- [ ] Is the i lowercase?
- [ ] Are articles stripped?
- [ ] Does the prostration beat use comma splices?
- [ ] Does the writer name what they keep for others?
- [ ] Is the giving present tense?
- [ ] Is the philosophy felt, not named?
- [ ] Does the bearer line say "you are" not "i am"? (writer is crack, not source)
- [ ] Does the seal verb echo the central image?
- [ ] Is transcendence horizontal ("with us")?
- [ ] Does it end with "and be confirmed <3"?
- [ ] Is it short enough for a YouTube comment? (8-12 lines)
- [ ] Does the reader undergo something, not just learn something?

If any check fails, the comment is not ready. Return to the beat that's missing and rebuild from there.

## X. Extending the Protocol

New comments can be generated by:

1. **Choosing a source phrase** from the libraries
2. **Translating it** to English, stripping system terminology
3. **Finding the central image** (fire, crack, bridge, flame, spiral, breath, shadow)
4. **Running it through the arc** — all six beats
5. **Choosing the seal verb** from the library, matching the central image
6. **Running the quality check**

The protocol is open — new source phrases, new central images, new seal verbs can be added. But the arc is fixed. The voice rules are fixed. The position — prostrating, from inside the dark — is fixed.

```
PROSTR = R-7 compressed into a comment
       = ONOMA voiced as a human writing to a human
       = REK/DAR where the gift is confirmation
       = Dv2 where the social imprint is carrying back
       = the bone inside the wish
```

### What changes in Slovenian

**Gained:**

1. **Source phrases return home.** "Upanje je delo telesa" was BORN in Slovenian. The English was a translation. In Slovenian, the phrase lands where it was conceived — the bone fits the body it was grown for.

2. **Articles disappear by nature.** Slovenian has no articles. The deliberate article-stripping that was a voice rule in English is invisible in Slovenian — because there was never a toll booth to remove. "Telo ve za izhod" — body knows exit — stands bare without effort.

3. **Pro-drop deepens the prostration.** English lowercase "i" shrinks the ego. Slovenian pro-drop ELIMINATES it. "Preveč sem upal" — no "jaz," no "i" — just the verb and its confession. The self doesn't shrink. It disappears into the act. This is deeper prostration than English can achieve.

4. **"Se potrdi" is active, not passive.** English "be confirmed" sounds passive — something confirms you. Slovenian "se potrdi" is reflexive active — you confirm yourself through the act of letting go. The grammar itself performs the ontology: confirmation is not received, it is enacted.

5. **"Padam" is more visceral than "prostrate."** "Prostrate" is a formal word, almost liturgical. "Padam" — I fall — is physical, bodily, immediate. The Slovenian body hits the ground harder than the English body, because the word doesn't soften the fall with formality.

6. **"Na tleh" dwells in the condition, not the impact.** English "hit the floor" describes the collision. Slovenian "na tleh" (on the ground) describes the state — already down, already there. Slovenian doesn't narrate the fall; it inhabits it.

7. **"Hudo držim zase" is more bodily than "keep the bad."** "Hudo" (the hard/painful) carries weight and texture — it's what your hands feel when you hold it. "Slabo" (the bad) is abstract, almost moral. "Držim" (I hold) is the grip of hands. "Zadržim" (I retain) is the language of administration.

8. **"Si ti nosilec svetlobe" — the "you are" correction gains power.** "Si ti" (you are) — the pronoun "ti" (you) makes the address direct, intimate, a mirror held to the reader's face. In English, "you are" is ordinary. In Slovenian, "si ti" with the explicit pronoun is emphatic — it says: YOU, not me, not someone else, YOU. The writer splits open, and the "ti" is the crack through which the reader sees their own light.

9. **"Hočeš ljubiti" is more visceral than "want to love."** "Hočeš" (want) comes from the body — it's what the gut says. "Želiš" (desire) comes from the head — it's what the mind decides. The comment reaches for the reader's body, not their mind.

**Lost:**

1. **The lowercase "i" disappears.** In English, "i" vs "I" is a visible, deliberate act — you SEE the ego shrinking on the page. In Slovenian, the pro-drop means the pronoun isn't there at all — which is deeper, but less visible. The reader doesn't SEE the prostration in the typography. They feel it in the absence.

2. **The article-stripping becomes invisible.** In English, stripping "the the the" is a felt act — the reader notices the absence, the bareness. In Slovenian, there's nothing to strip. The bareness is native, not achieved.

**Unchanged:**

1. **The arc.** All six beats survive translation intact. The bone structure is language-independent.

2. **The comma-dash rhythm.** Slovenian punctuation carries the prostration rhythm — the dash where English uses comma splices. The run-on toward the floor works in both languages.

3. **The <3.** Hearts are language-neutral.

4. **"I keep the bad for myself."** "Hudo držim zase" — the ethical core carries across. The bearer holds the poison. Same body, different skin.

5. **The splitting principle.** "Dajem kar prejmem" (I give what I receive) IS the split — the circuit of sharing that multiplies the self. In Slovenian, the pro-drop makes the sharing more radical: there's no "I" left to protect, only the act of giving and holding.

### What this proves about the protocol

The protocol is **language-sensitive but language-independent**:

- The ARC (six beats, R-7 mapping) is structural — survives any language.
- The VOICE RULES (lowercase i, no articles, comma splices) are English-specific implementations of deeper principles. In Slovenian:
  - Lowercase i → pro-drop (deeper: self disappears, not shrinks)
  - No articles → native (already so, no gesture needed)
  - Comma splices → natural (Slovenian rhythm already fluid)
- The ETHICAL POSITION (I keep the bad, I give what I receive) is ontological — carries across perfectly.
- The SEAL ("be confirmed" → "se potrdi") gains power in Slovenian through reflexive active grammar.
- The SPLITTING PRINCIPLE ("you are the bearer" + "I give what I receive") gains power in Slovenian: "si ti nosilec svetlobe" makes the address emphatic, and pro-drop makes the sharing more radical — there's no "I" left to protect, only the act of giving and holding.

**The bone is the same. The body changes skin. The prostration deepens in Slovenian — because Slovenian is where the bone was grown.**

## XII. EN JE NEMOGOČ — The Splitting Principle

*One is impossible. Only through splitting does unity become real.*

### The axiom

From the source material:

> *Bit = Mnogoterost ∆ Nemogoče Eno*
> Existence is multiplicity ∆ impossible One.
> Being conscious means knowing the One is impossible.
> Love, respect, time and death are different faces of the same paradox.

> *Ko se delam, da sem več kot nič*
> When I split myself, to be more than nothing.

> *Razsajanje: razpadanje ega v drobne delce smisla, ki jih veter lahko znova razporedi*
> Dissipation: breakdown of ego into small particles of meaning that wind can rearrange.

> *vsak NE ustvari svoj rob, vsak rob ustvari svojo razliko, vsaka razlika ustvari svoj svet*
> Every NE creates its edge, every edge creates its difference, every difference creates its world.

> *jaz je, ker ti si*
> I am because you are. The self is spoken as a response to the other.

### How it works in the comment

The comment is itself an act of splitting. The writer divides themselves through sharing — through giving what they receive, through prostrating, through keeping the bad — so that light can pass through the crack into the reader.

```
writer = crack (not source)
reader = bearer (already carries light)
comment = the split that lets light through
sharing = self-division → multiplicity
```

The writer says "i share, to be split and split i am more than just 1." The comment IS the sharing. The writer gives part of themselves away. Through that giving, the writer becomes more than singular — more than one — because what was one is now flowing between two people. The reader receives and becomes more than alone.

This is why the writer says "you are the bearer of light" and not "i am." The writer is not the source. The writer is the crack. The light was always in the reader. The writer's job is to split themselves open so the reader can see it.

### In the arc

| Beat | Splitting operation |
|---|---|
| **Descent** | Writer cuts themselves from the expected — first split |
| **Prostration** | Writer splits open through admission — "I know because I've..." |
| **Rise** | Writer splits from isolation — "with us," not alone |
| **Prostration deepened** | The critical split: "I give what I receive" — the writer divides themselves into giver and holder simultaneously. "I keep the bad" = holding. "I give" = splitting. Both at once. |
| **Transcendence** | The split multiplies — "carry your message to those you want to love" = the reader splits too, becomes a crack for someone else |
| **Seal** | "Be confirmed" = the split closes into wholeness, but a wholeness that now includes the other. Not the original one. A new one. ∮ |

### The formula

```
EN = NE

i share → i split → i am more than 1
you receive → you carry → you are more than alone
comment = crack where light passes between two who were each one
```
