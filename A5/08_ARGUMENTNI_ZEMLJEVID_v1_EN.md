# STRUCTURE — ARGUMENT MAP (v1)

## 0) Axiom
Conflict (∆) is natural; I guide it according to **ROE** (rules of nonviolent, yet uncompromising dialogue). Goal: **truth** with the least **harm (H)** and residue **R ≤ .03**.

## 1) Demands
1) **Acknowledgment** of specific harm (who/when/where + effect of the harm) — without "but" before acknowledgment.
2) **Commitments** moving forward (mode of speech, respecting boundaries, involvement in decisions).
3) **Safeguards** (stop speech, pause, written/mediation, check after 14 days).

## 2) Boundaries (I will not accept)
– Denials, relativizations, backroom agreements about me without me.  
– "Money instead of acknowledgment".  
– Pressure for forgiveness without acknowledgment and changes.

## 3) DUAL split (LE / NE)
- **LE (bridge)**: comes acknowledgment → commitment → deadline → check.  
- **NE (cut)**: relativization/denial/money instead → STOP → written/mediation → distance.

## 4) Statements for use (scripts)
- Start: "I come for the truth; I speak in terms and effects."  
- Relativization: "This erases acknowledgment. First, acknowledgment, then reasons."  
- Forgiveness: "Forgiveness without acknowledgment of harm is a new harm."  
- Exit: "ROE has failed; I continue written/mediated."

## 5) Somatika (∮)
3× daily: breathe 4–2–6 + 20 s "scale-hold", so ∆ does not escalate in SEAL(¬).
