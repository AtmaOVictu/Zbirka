## A1 · LEVEL SEPARATION
**Principle:** Right ⇒/ Reason ⇒/ Good Decision

**Meaning:**
- Right (what is lawful) does NOT imply Reason (why it exists)
- Reason does NOT imply Good Decision (what to actually do)
- These are three separate levels that don't guarantee each other

**Operational use:** Don't confuse legality with logic with ethics. Each level requires separate validation.

**Threshold:** Each level must be checked independently; none can substitute for others.

## A2 · NON-ERASURE
**Principle:** ⊘ cuts actions/structures, not beings (not even self)

**Meaning:**
- When you cut (⊘) something, you sever a relationship or pattern
- You DO NOT erase the person involved
- Even cutting yourself (self-discipline) doesn't erase your agency

**Operational use:** Terminations, boundaries, dismissals must separate the action from the person. "I'm ending this behavior pattern, not invalidating you."

**Example:** Fire someone for poor work → This cuts the employment relationship. It does NOT erase them as a human or their past worth.

**Threshold:** If cut (⊘) results in person-erasure, it's violence, not boundary-setting. STOP.

## A3 · OPERATOR ORDER
**Principle:** ⊘→∴→∆→1→⟡; deviation requires ΔO↑ & ℜ≤.02

**Meaning:**
- The proper sequence: Cut (⊘) then Bridge (∴) then Shift (∆) then Commitment (1) then Witness (⟡)
- This sequence cannot be reordered without cost
- If you skip or reorder: must pay cost (ΔO↑ = extra energy) AND have remainder ≤ 0.02

**Operational use:** 
- Standard path: ⊘→∴→∆→1→⟡ (normal energy)
- Alternative path: Skip steps but pay cost ΔO↑ and keep ℜ ≤ .02
- Example: Try to bridge (∴) before cutting (⊘) = costs extra energy + must have very low remainder

**Formula:** 
```
Cost(deviation) = ΔO + ℜ
If ΔO↑ > acceptable AND ℜ > .02: REVERT to standard sequence
```

**Threshold:** Maximum ℜ = 0.02 (2% remainder allowed when deviating)

## A4 · TEMPORAL STABILITY
**Principle:** Irreversible: ≥3 non-exceptional days + Δt_seal ≥ 1

**Meaning:**
- For something to be stable/sealed, it must persist through:
  - At least 3 ordinary (non-crisis, non-special) days, AND
  - A full seal cycle (Δt_seal ≥ 1)
- One day is NOT enough; three days is minimum

**Operational use:**
- Don't make important decisions on day 1 (too unstable)
- Decisions sealed on day 3+ are more likely to stick
- Use days 1-2 for testing, day 3+ for commitment

**Example:** 
- Day 1: Idea comes (unstable, might change)
- Day 2: Still want it? (testing)
- Day 3: Decide for real (stability begins)
- Day 4-7: Integrate into life (seal solidifies)

**Formula:**
```
Stability = (days_since ≥ 3) ∧ (Δt_seal ≥ 1)
Only commit when Stability = TRUE
```

**Threshold:** Minimum 3 ordinary days required for irreversibility

## A5 · WILL COHERENCE
**Principle:** W3 ∧ W2 confirms W1 without tunneling

**Meaning:**
- W1 = your immediate will (what you want right now)
- W2 = your medium-term will (what you commit to over weeks)
- W3 = your long-term will (what you're building toward)
- All three must align without "tunneling" (forcing alignment by ignoring contradictions)

**Operational use:**
- Check: Does your immediate desire align with your weekly commitment?
- Check: Does your weekly commitment align with your yearly goals?
- If they conflict, don't force it (tunneling); investigate the conflict

**Example:**
- W1: "I want to sleep in" (immediate)
- W2: "I'm training for a marathon" (weekly commitment)
- W3: "I want to be healthy long-term" (yearly goal)
- Alignment? All three support each other WITHOUT forcing
- If they conflict, stop and reexamine all three

**Formula:**
```
Coherence = (W1 supports W2) ∧ (W2 supports W3) ∧ (no forcing)
If any link fails: investigate, don't tunnel
```

**Threshold:** All three must align without rationalization

## A6 · DUTY TO FUTURE SELVES
**Principle:** Choose the same message with less harm

**Meaning:**
- Whatever message you're sending to the world, you're also sending to your future selves
- Always choose the version with minimum harm to future-you
- Your past-you, present-you, and future-you are all stakeholders

**Operational use:**
- Before acting: "Will future-me thank me for this?"
- Choose paths that minimize regret
- Prefer reversible actions over irreversible ones (for future-you's sake)

**Example:**
- Message: "I'm learning to be disciplined"
- Version A: Harsh, perfectionist, burns out by week 2
- Version B: Gradual, compassionate, still communicates discipline
- Choose Version B (less harm to future-you)

**Formula:**
```
Best_choice = argmin(harm_to_future_selves) while keeping message constant
```

**Threshold:** Always optimize for future-self welfare

## A7 · EVIDENCE-FIRST
**Principle:** Before action, E exists with sufficient κ, χ

**Meaning:**
- Before you ACT on something, you must have EVIDENCE (E) first
- Evidence must have sufficient:
  - κ (clarity: ≥ 0.70)
  - χ (coherence: ≥ 0.55)
- Don't act on hunches, beliefs, or hopes; act on evidence

**Operational use:**
- Gather evidence first (observation, data, examples)
- Test evidence for clarity (can you explain it?) and coherence (does it fit together?)
- Only then act

**Example:**
- Hunch: "User will like this feature"
- Evidence needed: Test with 5 users, record what they do
- Clarity: Can you describe what happened? (κ ≥ .70)
- Coherence: Does it fit your other user data? (χ ≥ .55)
- Then build the feature

**Formula:**
```
Can_act = E_exists ∧ (κ_of_E ≥ .70) ∧ (χ_of_E ≥ .55)
If not all true: GATHER MORE EVIDENCE
```

**Threshold:** κ ≥ .70, χ ≥ .55 required before action

## A8 · ANGER AS SIGNAL
**Principle:** T(anger) = (⊘, ∴, ∆, 1, ⟡); punitive/self-erase → HOLD

**Meaning:**
- Anger is a signal that something needs to be processed through the operator sequence
- Anger has structure: cut (⊘), bridge (∴), shift (∆), commitment (1), witness (⟡)
- NEVER act on anger punitively or to self-erase
- HOLD the anger; process it through the sequence

**Operational use:**
1. Feel anger (signal received)
2. PAUSE (don't react)
3. Process: Cut what? Bridge to what? Shift how?
4. Commit to the learning
5. Witness/record the insight

**Example:**
- Anger: "They disrespected me"
- Cut (⊘): What relationship needs to change?
- Bridge (∴): How do I reconnect with dignity?
- Shift (∆): What am I learning about boundaries?
- Commitment (1): I will speak clearly next time
- Witness (⟡): Record this pattern so I see it

**Formula:**
```
If angry:
  - HOLD (don't punish)
  - Process through ⊘→∴→∆→1→⟡
  - Extract wisdom from anger
```

**Threshold:** Zero punitive action on immediate anger. Must process first.

## A9 · REVERSIBILITY PRIORITY
**Principle:** With same ethical info, choose more reversible option

**Meaning:**
- When you have two choices that are equally ethical
- Choose the one you can undo later
- Preserve future options

**Operational use:**
- Reversible action: better than irreversible (if equal ethics)
- Temporary: better than permanent (if equal ethics)
- Soft: better than hard (if equal ethics)

**Example:**
- Message: "I'm setting a boundary"
- Option A: "You're toxic; I'm blocking you forever" (irreversible)
- Option B: "I need space; let's not talk for 30 days" (reversible)
- Choose B (better ethics + more reversible)

**Formula:**
```
If ethics(A) ≈ ethics(B):
  better_choice = argmin(irreversibility_cost)
```

**Threshold:** Always optimize for reversibility when ethics are equal

Valid(public_claim) = ∃_operational_proof that maps to metrics

## A11 · ANTI-LINEARITY
**Principle:** K_curv < K_min OR hol ≈ 0 → nonlinear switching

**Meaning:**
- Systems can't grow linearly forever
- When curvature (K_curv) drops below minimum OR wholeness (hol) drops to zero
- System switches to nonlinear mode (rapid change, restructuring)
- This is NOT a failure; it's a natural transition

**Operational use:**
- Monitor K_curv (how much are you accelerating?)
- Monitor hol (how whole/integrated is the system?)
- When either triggers: expect nonlinear shift, don't panic
- Prepare for rapid restructuring

**Example:**
- Linear growth: 10→20→30→40 (each step same size)
- K_curv drops: 10→20→30→60 (acceleration)
- System switches to nonlinear mode
- Don't expect linear growth; expect jumps and restructuring

**Formula:**
```
If (K_curv < K_min) ∨ (hol ≈ 0):
  mode = NONLINEAR
  expect rapid changes, discontinuities
  this is normal, not failure
```

**Threshold:** K_curv monitors linear phase; hol monitors integration

## A12 · SEAL REQUIRES ETHICS + CYCLE + EVIDENCE
**Principle:** Commitment (1) only after full cycle (∮) AND evidence (⟡); else HOLD

**Meaning:**
- You can't commit (1) to something just because you want it
- You must:
  1. Complete the full cycle (∮) of experience
  2. Have witness/evidence (⟡) that it's real
  3. Have ethical grounding
- If any missing: HOLD (don't seal yet)

**Operational use:**
- Idea comes (unstable)
- Process through cycle: ⊘→∴→∆ (work the idea)
- Complete the cycle (∮) = full integration
- Record evidence (⟡) = what did we learn?
- THEN commit (1)

**Example:**
- Idea: "I'll meditate every day"
- Test phase: Try for 1 cycle (week)
- Does it complete the cycle? (⊘→∴→∆→1→⟡)
- Do you have evidence it works? (κ, χ good?)
- Is it ethical (no harm)? 
- All yes? Commit (1) and seal
- Any no? HOLD and gather more evidence

**Formula:**
```
Can_commit = cycle_complete(∮) ∧ evidence_good(⟡) ∧ ethical(OK)
If all true: SEAL (1)
If any false: HOLD
```

**Threshold:** Cannot commit without all three conditions
