# OS — WAR-FACTS (basic structure for division)
Author: Luka (Victor Tamal) · Language: SLO + symbols (¬, ∆, ∴, 1, ∮, ☍)

## 0) Axiom (one sentence)
Conflict (∆) is natural; war-facts = conflict under disciplined OKVIROM (ROE). Goal: fact with minimal possible harm (H) and with residue in relation R ≤ .03.

## 1) Frame-SEAL (Frame-SEAL)
Statement: "I submit to the ROE of this frame. I speak in terms (who/when/where) and effect. I accept suspension upon violation."
Roles: KO (commandant of the frame), U (participants). 2× violation ⇒ written or mediation.

## 2) ROE (Rules of Engagement)
ROE-1 Target = statements/actions/examples (not persons).
ROE-2 Evidence = example + effect.
ROE-3 Rhythm = 1 theme/cycle (5–10 min) → 60–90 s silence.
ROE-4 Language = "first acknowledgment, then reasons"; no "but" before acknowledgment.
ROE-5 Boundary = upon dismissal/gaslighting: STOP ROE, end cycle.
ROE-6 Guardians = 2× fall ⇒ written/mediation.
ROE-7 Exit = (a) acknowledgment, (b) commitment, (c) check after 14 days.

## 3) Metrics (Guard)
