# Letter to the family — sharp version (v3)

Core: without **acknowledgment of harm** and **commitment to move forward**, I will not open new cycles with you. Money is not a topic without acknowledgment. Forgiveness is not a topic without acknowledgment. Denial/relativization → **STOP ROE**, transition to written/mediation.

**Three demands**
1) Clear acknowledgment of specific facts (who/when/where + impact).
2) Commitment to specific changes (communication, boundaries, inclusion).
3) Protective measures (stop-sentence, cooling-off period, written/mediation, check after 14 days).

**Statements for ongoing**
– "First, acknowledgment, then reasons."
– "Forgiveness without acknowledgment of harm is a new harm."
– "ROE has fallen. I will continue written or with a mediator."
