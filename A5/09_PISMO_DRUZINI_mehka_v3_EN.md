# Letter to the family — soft version (v3)

I'm writing because I've felt invisible and excluded from the decisions that shaped me. I want a **war-like** (but peaceful) conversation about the facts.

**What affected me**
1) Peace was chosen over the issue.  
2) We suggested money or "let it pass" instead of acknowledging the harm.  
3) Decisions were made about me without me; when I spoke up, the problem was "my tone".

**What's next**
– **First, acknowledgment**: what was wrong, when, and how it affected me (without "but").  
– **Commitments**: what will change (way of speaking, respect for boundaries, inclusion in decisions).  
– **Protective measures**: if the conversation stalls, we stop and continue in writing or with a mediator.

This is not an ultimatum. It's a framework that protects everyone. If we can't do this, I'll stay away — out of concern for myself, not out of anger.
