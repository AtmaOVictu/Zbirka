# VBL — Proposed §9 Amendment
## Ethics, accountability, and verifiable remediation of harm

**Status:** PROPOSAL FOR ALIGNMENT · UNCONFIRMED · NOT INCORPORATED INTO THE CANON

This proposal is published for public review and critical scrutiny. Publication does not mean that the text has been adopted, legally validated, or integrated into an implementation.

### §9.1 — Accountability is evidenced by actions
No one can establish ethical conduct merely by citing intentions, position, authority, or their own account of their conduct. Where consequences can be checked, actions, relevant evidence, resulting consequences, and verification limits should be recorded.

### §9.2 — Equal standards, different responsibilities
All participants have equal dignity. Evidentiary standards must be applied consistently regardless of a participant’s position. Different powers may create different responsibilities, but do not automatically confer credibility or exemption from scrutiny.

### §9.3 — Preventing and reducing harm
Before an action with reasonably foreseeable risk, assess possible consequences, select proportionate safeguards, and identify an accountable person. If risk becomes unacceptable, pause, limit, or re-authorize the action after a documented review.

### §9.4 — Right to challenge
Where feasible and safe, a person affected by an action or decision must have an opportunity to challenge relevant data, report harm, and submit contrary evidence. An objection must not be rejected solely because of the objector’s position, status, or disagreement with the decision-maker.

### §9.5 — Correction without erasing history
When an error is found, the responsible party must record the error, reason for correction, time of change, and link to the original record. The original should remain preserved unless a lawful deletion or data-protection requirement calls for a different process. Even then, where lawful and safe, an appropriate audit trail should be retained without unnecessary exposure of personal data.

### §9.6 — Independent verification
A claim of independent verification must identify the reviewer, scope, evidence, limitations, and any conflict of interest. The implementer’s own statement is not sufficient proof of independence. If independence cannot be confirmed, the status must be explicitly marked unconfirmed.

### §9.7 — Acknowledgment and remediation
A response to a discovered error must distinguish acknowledgment, record correction, remediation of consequences, and prevention of recurrence. Completing one step does not automatically prove the others were completed.

### §9.8 — No false claims of compliance
No entity may declare compliance solely on the basis of its own assertion, tests that were not run, an incomplete evidence trail, or an unavailable independent review. Failures, uncertainty, objections, and unresolved deficiencies must remain visible.

### §9.9 — Proportionality, privacy, and dignity
Verification must be sufficient for its purpose but must not collect or disclose more personal data than necessary. Privacy must not be used to conceal misconduct; demands for evidence must not be used to expose people without justification.

### §9.10 — Completion criteria
An obligation is complete only when the required evidence has been collected, checked, and linked to the result; identified errors have been addressed; and remaining unmet requirements are clearly stated. If verification is impossible, record that as a limitation, not a success.

## Requested independent review

Please provide concrete feedback on:
1. legal and terminological precision;
2. consistency with the existing canon and other §9 provisions;
3. feasibility in software and operational processes;
4. measurable acceptance criteria and negative/edge-case tests;
5. privacy, security, record authenticity, and resistance to circumvention.

Suggested edge cases include a forged source, duplicate identifier, missing timestamp, falsely named reviewer, unauthorized access through a proxy, deletion of the original, concealed disagreement, and a correction that is logged but not actually implemented. A checksum alone does not prove the truth or provenance of content.

**Verification status:** this proposal does not itself establish compliance. Tests must run against the actual implementation, raw results must be preserved, limitations stated, and reviewer independence checked. Legal validity, consistency of the complete canon, and independent audit remain unconfirmed until separately evidenced.

Please suggest amendments, state objections, and provide evidence. Blind endorsement is not requested. Originals, failures, and disagreements should remain traceable; no proposal should be represented as adopted canon without appropriate approval.
