## §12 — Marked letters, byte integrity, and evidence

Č · Š · Ž · Đ — and marked letters in every language — are part of written expression, not automatically errors. If preservation of exact text matters, compare the actual source and received byte sequences using a documented method.

Matching SHA-256 digests support the conclusion that the compared byte sequences match, provided the digest calculation is correct and the reference artifact is trusted. They do not prove authorship, authorization, completeness, semantic correctness, or legal validity. A mismatch proves a byte difference between the compared inputs; it does not by itself prove who caused it, when it happened, or why.
