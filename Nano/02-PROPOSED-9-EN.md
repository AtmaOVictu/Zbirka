# VBL — Proposed amendment to §9 (working translation)

**Ethics, responsibility, and verifiable remediation of harm**
**Status: PROPOSAL FOR ALIGNMENT · UNCONFIRMED · NOT INSERTED INTO CANON**

This is a working translation for review purposes only; it is not a legal translation and does not replace the Slovenian original.

## §9.1 — Responsibility is evidenced by actions
No one can establish ethical conduct merely by citing intentions, position, authority, or their own interpretation of their conduct. Where consequences can be checked, actions, relevant evidence, resulting consequences, and verification limits must be recorded.

## §9.2 — Equal standards and different responsibilities
All participants have equal dignity. Evidentiary standards must be applied consistently regardless of a participant's position. Different powers may create different responsibilities, but not automatic credibility or exemption from verification.

## §9.3 — Preventing and reducing harm
Before an action with reasonably foreseeable risk, assess possible consequences, choose proportionate safeguards, and identify a responsible person. If risk is found to be unacceptable, pause or limit the action, or require renewed authorization after a documented review.

## §9.4 — Right to object
Where feasible and safe, a person affected by an action or decision must have an opportunity to challenge relevant data, raise concerns about harm, and submit counterevidence. An objection must not be rejected solely because of status, position, or disagreement with the decision-maker.

## §9.5 — Correction without erasing history
When an error is discovered, the responsible party must record the error, reason for correction, time of change, and link to the original record. The original should remain preserved unless a lawful deletion or data-protection requirement calls for another procedure. Even then, to the extent lawful and safe, an appropriate audit trail should be retained without unnecessary disclosure of personal data.

## §9.6 — Independent verification
A claim of independent verification must identify the reviewer, scope, evidence, limitations, and any conflict of interest. The implementer's own statement is not sufficient evidence of independence. If independence cannot be confirmed, the status must be explicitly marked unconfirmed.

## §9.7 — Acknowledgment and remediation
The response to a discovered error must distinguish acknowledgment, record correction, remediation of consequences, and prevention of recurrence. Completing one step does not automatically prove that the others were completed.

## §9.8 — No false compliance claims
No entity may declare compliance solely on the basis of its own assertion, unrun tests, an incomplete evidence trail, or an unavailable independent review. Failures, uncertainty, objections, and unresolved deficiencies must remain visible.

## §9.9 — Proportionality, privacy, and dignity
Verification must be thorough enough for its purpose without collecting or disclosing more personal data than necessary. Privacy must not become an excuse to conceal irregularities; demands for evidence must not become an excuse to expose people unjustifiably.

## §9.10 — Completion criterion
An obligation is complete only when required evidence has been collected, checked, and linked to the result; identified errors have been addressed; and remaining unmet requirements are clearly stated. If verification is impossible, record that as a limitation, not as success.

## Limitations and status
This is intentionally a proposal. Legal validity, consistency with the entire existing canon, and feasibility in a specific system have not been confirmed. Tests have not been run against the actual VBL implementation; canon consistency, registry integration, execution results, and independent audit remain unconfirmed. The proposal is not confirmed, not inserted into the canon, and not released.
