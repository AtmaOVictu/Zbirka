## Email / direct invitation

**Subject:** Invitation to independently review VBL §9 — accountability and verifiable remediation

Hello,

I’m sharing a proposed amendment to §9 of VBL (Verification Before Assertion) for critical, independent review. It addresses accountability through evidence, consistent evidentiary standards, harm prevention, the right to challenge, corrections without erasing history, independent verification, remediation, truthful compliance claims, privacy, and completion criteria.

The text is explicitly a proposal: it has not been adopted into the canon, legally validated, or verified against a complete production implementation. Please identify concrete defects, counterexamples, legal or technical risks, and tests that could falsify claims of compliance. Agreement is not expected; evidence-based criticism is welcome.

The proposal and current distribution-status record are attached. Please do not treat publication or a checksum as proof that the content is true, legally enforceable, or implemented.

Thank you for considering an independent review.
