# Proposed clarifications — not yet adopted

These are drafting proposals for review. They are not represented as part of VBL 2.6, and they should not be silently inserted into that version.

## A. §7 / §9 — permission hierarchy

> Any written permission under this section grants only rights the author is entitled to grant. No permission, waiver, silence, interpretation, or later version authorizes a use prohibited by §9. All permissions remain subject to the ethical floor stated in §9.

Purpose: remove the direct conflict between “permission overrides any clause” and §9's express refusal of permission to degrade the universal state.

## B. §4 — cumulative cure conditions

> The conditions in this section are cumulative. For every named fall, restoration requires publication of the fall, attribution of affected authors, and preservation of the correction record. Where a fall is classified as predatory under §9, these conditions are necessary but not sufficient. Restoration additionally requires documented remediation of gains attributable to the degrading use, to the extent applicable and legally permissible. The record must state the evidence, corrective action, and result of independent verification. Findings concern identified acts, consequences, and documented patterns, not the intrinsic worth of a person or being.

Purpose: make the general cure and the additional predatory-fall condition cumulative. The terms “predatory”, “gains”, “remediation”, and “independent verification” still need definitions and a dispute procedure.

## C. §10 — version policy decision required

Do not insert a universal “latest version replaces all prior grants” formula until the rights policy is chosen. Select and state one policy, subject to legal review:

1. **Prospective policy:** new versions apply to copies or uses first licensed under that version; grants already made remain governed by their recorded terms, subject to their own termination provisions.
2. **Acceptance policy:** a new version applies to an existing recipient only when that recipient affirmatively accepts the new terms or takes a clearly specified action that validly establishes acceptance.

A repository may identify one current preferred version while still preserving the historical text and evidence for previous grants. That is distinct from extinguishing all older grants.

## D. §0 / §6 — licensed scope and excluded corpus

> The license applies only to the works and files identified as covered works in the release manifest. The private corpus expressly listed as excluded remains outside this license regardless of storage location. Third-party materials remain subject to their own rights and notices.

Purpose: distinguish legal scope from repository location. The covered-work list and excluded corpus inventory must be supplied before this can be finalized.

## E. §12 — scoped byte-identity claim

> Byte identity is asserted only for the canonical artifact and the transfers listed in the release evidence manifest. For each tested transfer, the source and destination byte sequences must have matching SHA-256 digests. Textual or visual equivalence without matching bytes must be reported as a different result, not byte identity.

Purpose: turn a universal claim into a repeatable testable claim without removing the principle that character marks are meaningful.
